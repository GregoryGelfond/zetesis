# Grouped source exclusions

The eager original-theory emitter recognizes a headless constraint whose body
is one comparison `X != Y` and positive flat atoms. The two variables are
distinct original slots. Every other original variable forms a shared key K.
Each atom refers either to K alone, K and X, or K and Y; none may read both X
and Y. The X and Y occurrence multisets must agree exactly after swapping the
lane variable, including predicate sign, arity, constants and repeated slots.
All declared slots must occur in an admitted positive atom. Generated binding
plans, structural patterns, other comparisons, default negation, aggregates,
conditionals and projections use the ordinary route.

This certificate describes the full unequal product of one positive relation
with itself for each K. It does not recognize an encoding or assume that atoms
in possible support are true. Lowered-body normalization has already combined
identical written literals while retaining their source origins; the certificate
compares the occurrences in that lowered body.

## Binding identity and formula meaning

A lane is a distinct typed value of X within one exact typed K. It is never a
formula ID. The compiler uses its existing term-coordinate owner for K and X,
with two temporary coordinate maps for lanes and groups. Numeric, symbolic and
constructed values retain their ordinary distinct identities. Different K
values never share a group.

Within one lane, complete condition witnesses combine by disjunction (Any).
The common condition G remains outside a native unit-weight threshold at two:
`not (G and count(lane conditions) >= 2)`. G may be derived and need not be a
fact. If two different lanes have the same condition q, the native row retains
two occurrences of q: replacing them by one would incorrectly allow q to hold.
Repeated witnesses of the same lane, in contrast, do not add count units.

`GroupedWitnessExclusion.witnessed_fibers_original` proves the equality of the
complete negative source family and grouped constraints. Its frozen and stable
corollaries preserve every candidate/tested-interpretation reading and stable
membership in unchanged context. They do not require the tested interpretation
to be a subset of the candidate. Rust must establish the full product premise,
typed distinctness, native occurrence multiplicity and concrete row semantics;
the laws do not verify the source recognizer or executable implementation.

## Traversal, provenance and publication

Two fresh cursors borrow the same completed Support and immutable component
plan. The first traversal records only typed K/lane identities. It names no
atoms, formula nodes or roots. If every group has fewer than two lanes, no
second traversal is needed. Otherwise, a second traversal constructs fresh
lane and common conditions, skipping singleton groups before atom naming.
No binding frame survives a cursor advance.

Every named lane has a distinct partner in its own group, so it occurs in an
original unequal pair. Symmetry makes the right-lane atom population the same
as the left-lane population. Common atoms are named only for groups with an
actual pair. This preserves the semantic atom set and each atom's first source
rule, including when a singleton group's common atom appears in a later rule.
Every emitted group root carries the original constraint's complete origins.
The relevant statement order is the prepared Program's canonical order, not
lexical source order: within one part, literal-head producers precede falsum-head
constraints. A separate Builder-level constraint-first test exercises delayed
producer naming without claiming that schedule is normal compiler order.
Raw atom/node/root positions, root multiplicity and traversal order may change;
so may first witnesses and interrupted search prefixes. Complete enumeration
and the reduct remain authoritative.

Roots are emitted only after the entire second traversal succeeds. Emission of
several groups is sequential through the ordinary root boundary, not a rollback
transaction. A later failure may leave earlier private nodes or roots, but no
successful compiled theory escapes. Missing lane/guard observations are typed
owner failures. Once grouping begins, a resource or owner failure never retries
the ordinary pair join. Only a declined source grammar selects that route.

## Work and storage

The existing substitution counter measures actual completed one-lane rows. For
R completed rows in the first traversal, the complete positive case performs R
substitutions if there is no eligible group, and 2R otherwise. The existing
substitution allowance applies cumulatively; unperformed pair substitutions
are not charged. Every grammar read, semantic identity comparison, actual join,
coordinate-map search/move, condition fold and native publication retains its
ordinary work/cancellation accounting. The production polling policy is
unchanged, while the amount and sequence of actual work may change.

The copied source plan, component plan, current cursor, coordinate maps and
native row buffers have their actual headers and capacities charged to the
existing support-byte allowance. Canonical tuple entries retained by the one
existing term owner remain live after temporary maps retire. Requested and
actual allocation observations remain distinct. In-place coordinate-map
insertion can move earlier entries; avoiding the pair product is not a claim
that all grouping work is linear. No new allowance is introduced, and aggregate
cache-row/element units are not used to account for grouped constraints.

Grammar screening itself can consume work and storage before declining an
unsupported rule. A refusal at that point remains a refusal. Lowered syntax,
first-pass coordinates and private second-pass formula construction can
therefore change tight-limit and error-order boundaries. Failed work remains
reported, and interruption never establishes inconsistency or completeness.

The canonical tests compare original and arbitrary frozen truth, typed atom
identity and per-atom source metadata, root origins, duplicate native conditions,
unsupported grammar, exact work/substitution boundaries and owner storage.
Their injected every-charge cancellation control establishes typed stopping;
it does not establish equality of historical production polling positions.
