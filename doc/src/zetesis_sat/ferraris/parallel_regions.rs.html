1//! Regions walked by several workers at once, sharing immutable preparation,
2//! a cumulative allowance and a work-stealing frontier of regions still to visit.
3//!
4//! Each worker owns a work-stealing deque of regions with their knowledge, a
5//! lease on the enumeration's shared allowance that serves its consecutive
6//! regions, and its own evaluation workspace. Candidate and reduct traversals
7//! share the authenticated original-theory index but never their mutable
8//! knowledge. A worker pops a region from its own deque, narrows it from the
9//! knowledge it carries, drops it when refuted, splits it otherwise and pushes
10//! both children back onto its deque; a worker whose deque is empty steals a
11//! region from a peer. At a leaf it decides membership as the scalar proposer
12//! does, by the class certificate when one applies and else by the
13//! proper-subset query as a region tree; a stable model is sent to the
14//! enumeration. The regions partition the candidate space exactly
15//! (`Cube.split_partition`, `Cube.split_disjoint` of `Search.lean`), so no leaf
16//! is visited by two workers and every leaf by one, whatever the interleaving;
17//! the order in which models arrive is the schedule's and is not a property of
18//! the result, and no two runs promise the same order.
19//!
20//! A restriction added while the workers run narrows the regions not yet
21//! visited: each worker reads the restrictions before a narrowing, and a
22//! region reached before a restriction existed gets fresh knowledge under
23//! it. A work, decision or candidate ceiling is shared: the first worker
24//! to exhaust it raises the stop, the others stop at their next charge or
25//! their next region, and the enumeration is incomplete. The models the
26//! workers verified before they stopped are delivered first and the stop
27//! after them, so a leaf admitted under the candidate ceiling is never
28//! lost to a worker the ceiling refused. A cancellation observed by the
29//! coordinator can end its pull immediately; explicit stop or drop joins any
30//! remaining workers. The region counts and reading
31//! work, the candidates, the countermodel counts and the phase timings are
32//! the workers' live counters, current while they run; the certificate and
33//! reduct receipts are merged when the workers have finished, so a snapshot
34//! taken earlier lacks them. Phase timings, when enabled, are the workers'
35//! own narrowing and leaf decisions summed over the workers, so they may
36//! exceed the wall time of the walk.
37//!
38//! Each deque has its own mutex: local removal and split publication lock only
39//! that deque, and a thief skips a busy peer. Narrowing, payload cloning, reduct
40//! checks and model sends hold no queue lock. Slot growth is fallible and occurs
41//! before either child is published. An atomic counter of unresolved regions
42//! carries termination — a split raises it before pushing the children, a
43//! resolved region lowers it once, and an unsuccessful thief stops only once
44//! that counter has reached zero. An atomic closed flag, set on the first stop
45//! or when the enumeration stops listening, halts the others at their next region.
46//! Idle workers wait at a gate that the last resolution and every close open, so
47//! none sleeps out its timed wait after the walk has ended; the timed wait bounds
48//! only how late an idle worker sees a cancellation or a newly published region.
49//!
50//! A local depth-first walk keeps at most one older sibling per ancestor and
51//! the two newest children. Each split decides another atom, and a worker steals
52//! only with an empty deque, so each deque holds at most `atom_count + 1` entries.
53//! Storage grows with the observed depth rather than reserving that bound for
54//! every worker. Capacity and payloads are released after joining. Fallible slot
55//! growth does not make region or knowledge cloning fallible.

76/// An idle worker looks for a region again and polls cooperative control at
77/// least once per timed wait; the end of the walk wakes it at once.
78const IDLE_WAIT: Duration = Duration::from_millis(1);

117/// What ends the walk — every region resolved, or a close — and the gate idle
118/// workers wait at for either.
119///
120/// The count and the flag are atomics because the region loop reads them for
121/// every region; the gate guards no data. Every transition that ends the walk
122/// changes its atomic first and then takes the gate to wake the waiters, and an
123/// idle worker takes the gate and re-checks both conditions before it waits.
124/// Whichever reaches the gate first, no idle worker waits past the end of the
125/// walk: a worker that takes the gate after the ending transition sees it, and
126/// one that took the gate before is already waiting when the wake comes. Zero
127/// is terminal, since a region in hand counts until it is resolved. Every wait
128/// stays bounded by its timeout, so correctness never rests on a wake: the
129/// timeout is how an idle worker sees a cancellation or a newly published
130/// region.
131struct Termination {
132    /// Created-but-unresolved regions across every worker's deque and hand: a
133    /// split adds one (before pushing its children), a refuted or decided
134    /// region subtracts one. It starts at one for the root and reaches zero
135    /// exactly when the whole frontier is resolved; a read of zero is a true
136    /// zero, so a worker that finds every deque empty and reads zero is done.
137    outstanding: AtomicUsize,
138    /// A stop was raised (error or cancellation) or the enumeration stopped
139    /// listening; workers check it before each region and while idle, and
140    /// exit. Normal termination is `outstanding` reaching zero.
141    closed: AtomicBool,
142    /// Held to re-check the walk's end before an idle wait, and to wake the
143    /// waiters after the walk ends.
144    gate: Mutex<()>,
145    /// Idle workers wait here for the walk to end.
146    idle: Condvar,
147}
148
149impl Termination {
150    /// The root is the one outstanding region until it is split.
151    fn new() -> Self {
152        Self {
153            outstanding: AtomicUsize::new(1),
154            closed: AtomicBool::new(false),
155            gate: Mutex::new(()),
156            idle: Condvar::new(),
157        }
158    }
159
160    /// A split replaces its parent by two children: count the net gain of one
161    /// before either child becomes visible.
162    fn grow(&self) {
163        self.outstanding.fetch_add(1, Ordering::AcqRel);
164    }
165
166    /// A refuted or decided region leaves the frontier. The last one ends the
167    /// walk and wakes every idle worker.
168    fn resolve(&self) {
169        let previous = self.outstanding.fetch_sub(1, Ordering::AcqRel);
170        debug_assert_ne!(previous, 0, "a region was resolved twice");
171        if previous == 1 {
172            self.wake_idle();
173        }
174    }
175
176    /// Close the walk: workers take no further region, and idle workers wake.
177    fn close(&self) {
178        self.closed.store(true, Ordering::Release);
179        self.wake_idle();
180    }
181
182    fn is_resolved(&self) -> bool {
183        self.outstanding.load(Ordering::Acquire) == 0
184    }
185
186    fn is_closed(&self) -> bool {
187        self.closed.load(Ordering::Acquire)
188    }
189
190    fn has_ended(&self) -> bool {
191        self.is_closed() || self.is_resolved()
192    }
193
194    /// Wait for the walk to end, for at most `timeout`, unless it already has.
195    /// A return does not mean the walk ended: the caller re-checks.
196    fn wait_idle(&self, timeout: Duration) {
197        let gate = self.gate.lock().unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner);
198        if !self.has_ended() {
199            drop(
200                self.idle
201                    .wait_timeout(gate, timeout)
202                    .unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner),
203            );
204        }
205    }
206
207    /// Wake every idle worker, after the transition that ended the walk.
208    fn wake_idle(&self) {
209        let _gate = self.gate.lock().unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner);
210        self.idle.notify_all();
211    }
212}

230/// One phase's calls and elapsed nanoseconds, summed over the workers.
231#[derive(Default)]
232struct LivePhase {
233    calls: AtomicU64,
234    nanos: AtomicU64,
235    /// Sticky measurement incompleteness, independent of semantic coverage.
236    overflowed: AtomicBool,
237}
238
239impl LivePhase {
240    /// Keep each counter monotone, saturating only after marking the
241    /// measurement incomplete. Live snapshots read independent atomics;
242    /// after workers join, the totals are exact or explicitly overflowed.
243    fn add(&self, counter: &AtomicU64, amount: u64) {
244        if amount == 0 {
245            return;
246        }
247        if counter
248            .fetch_update(Ordering::Relaxed, Ordering::Relaxed, |value| {
249                value.checked_add(amount)
250            })
251            .is_err()
252        {
253            self.overflowed.store(true, Ordering::Relaxed);
254            // Every writer only increases this counter. An overflow makes
255            // MAX absorbing, including concurrent successful additions.
256            counter.store(u64::MAX, Ordering::Relaxed);
257        }
258    }
259}
260
261impl Live {
262    fn add(counter: &AtomicU64, amount: u64) {
263        counter.fetch_add(amount, Ordering::Relaxed);
264    }
265    fn read(counter: &AtomicU64) -> u64 {
266        counter.load(Ordering::Relaxed)
267    }
268
269    /// Add what a worker measured since its last report.
270    fn add_timings(&self, before: &SearchPhaseTimings, after: &SearchPhaseTimings) {
271        for (live, (before, after)) in self.timings.iter().zip(phases(before).zip(phases(after))) {
272            // A worker can become incomplete without changing its retained
273            // numeric prefix, so propagate its flag even for a zero delta.
274            if before.overflowed || after.overflowed {
275                live.overflowed.store(true, Ordering::Relaxed);
276            }
277            let calls = after.calls.saturating_sub(before.calls);
278            let elapsed = after.elapsed.saturating_sub(before.elapsed);
279            let nanos = u64::try_from(elapsed.as_nanos()).unwrap_or_else(|_| {
280                live.overflowed.store(true, Ordering::Relaxed);
281                u64::MAX
282            });
283            live.add(&live.calls, calls);
284            live.add(&live.nanos, nanos);
285        }
286    }
287
288    fn timings(&self) -> SearchPhaseTimings {
289        let read = |live: &LivePhase| PhaseMeasurement {
290            calls: Self::read(&live.calls),
291            elapsed: Duration::from_nanos(Self::read(&live.nanos)),
292            overflowed: live.overflowed.load(Ordering::Relaxed),
293        };
294        let [
295            candidates,
296            original_validation,
297            reduct,
298            reduct_preparation,
299            certified,
300        ] = &self.timings;
301        SearchPhaseTimings {
302            candidates: read(candidates),
303            original_validation: read(original_validation),
304            reduct: read(reduct),
305            reduct_preparation: read(reduct_preparation),
306            certified: read(certified),
307        }
308    }
309}

312fn phases(timings: &SearchPhaseTimings) -> impl Iterator<Item = &PhaseMeasurement> {
313    [
314        &timings.candidates,
315        &timings.original_validation,
316        &timings.reduct,

323impl Shared {
324    fn queue(&self, index: usize) -> MutexGuard<'_, VecDeque<Entry>> {
325        self.queues[index]
326            .lock()
327            .unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner)
328    }
329
330    fn take_local(&self, index: usize) -> Option<Entry> {
331        self.queue(index).pop_back()
332    }
333
334    /// Prepare space for both children before the count or either queue entry
335    /// changes. The injected reservation is the sole fallible storage effect;
336    /// success must reserve `additional` slots, as `reserve_regions` does.
337    fn publish_split(
338        &self,
339        index: usize,
340        held: Entry,
341        cut: Entry,
342        reserve: impl FnOnce(&mut VecDeque<Entry>, usize) -> Result<(), Incomplete>,
343    ) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
344        let mut queue = self.queue(index);
345        reserve(&mut queue, 2)?;
346        // The parent is still outstanding. Count its net gain before making
347        // either child visible; the reserved pushes cannot allocate.
348        self.termination.grow();
349        queue.push_back(held);
350        queue.push_back(cut);
351        Ok(())
352    }
353
354    /// Close for a normal end of work (no error): workers exit when they next
355    /// check, idle ones at once, without recording a stop.
356    fn close(&self) {
357        self.termination.close();
358    }
359
360    /// Raise a stop: work closes, and the first stop is the one reported.
361    fn stop(&self, error: Incomplete) {
362        self.stopped
363            .lock()
364            .unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner)
365            .get_or_insert(error);
366        self.termination.close();
367    }
368
369    /// The first stop raised, if any.
370    fn stopped(&self) -> Option<Incomplete> {
371        *self.stopped.lock().unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner)
372    }
373}

406    /// Prepare the shared structure; the workers start on the first
407    /// proposal, once the enumeration's certificates are configured.
408    pub(crate) fn new(
409        theory: &Theory,
410        workers: NonZeroUsize,
411        limits: Limits,
412        cancellation: Cancellation,
413        budget: &mut Budget<'_>,
414    ) -> Result<Self, Incomplete> {
415        let super::regions::Opened {
416            producers,
417            index,
418            statistics,
419        } = super::regions::open(theory, budget)?;
420        let (sender, receiver) = sync_channel(workers.get() * CHANNEL_SLACK);
421        let root = (
422            Region::all_open(theory.atom_count()),
423            CandidateKnowledge::new(index.narrower().knowledge()),
424        );
425        let mut queues = crate::search::storage(workers.get())?;
426        let mut first = VecDeque::new();
427        reserve_regions(&mut first, 1)?;
428        first.push_back(root);
429        queues.push(Mutex::new(first));
430        for _ in 1..workers.get() {
431            queues.push(Mutex::new(VecDeque::new()));
432        }
433        Ok(Self {
434            shared: Arc::new(Shared {
435                producers,
436                index,
437                certificate: None,
438                filter: None,
439                restrictions: RwLock::new(Conditions::default()),
440                queues,
441                termination: Termination::new(),
442                stopped: Mutex::new(None),
443                budget: SharedBudget::new(limits.search, budget.statistics),
444                limits,
445                cancellation,
446                workers: workers.get(),
447                timed: false,
448                candidates: AtomicU64::new(0),
449                live: Live::default(),
450            }),
451            receiver: Some(receiver),
452            sender: Some(sender),
453            handles: Vec::new(),
454            started: false,
455            exhausted: false,
456            statistics,
457            merged: Statistics::default(),
458        })
459    }

550    /// Restrict every region not yet visited to the classical models of
551    /// `restriction`; the workers read it before their next narrowing.
552    /// Indexing it is charged to the shared allowance the workers draw on,
553    /// and the enumeration's counters take the charge at once.
554    pub(crate) fn restrict(
555        &mut self,
556        restriction: &Theory,
557        budget: &mut Budget<'_>,
558    ) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
559        if !self.started {
560            self.synchronize_budget(budget.statistics)?;
561        }
562        let narrower = Narrower::try_new(restriction).map_err(super::regions::stopped)?;
563        self.shared.budget.charge(narrower.work())?;
564        self.account(budget);
565        self.statistics.counts.work += narrower.work();
566        let mut restrictions = self
567            .shared
568            .restrictions
569            .write()
570            .unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner);
571        restrictions
572            .permanent
573            .try_reserve(1)
574            .map_err(|_| Incomplete::Allocation)?;
575        restrictions
576            .permanent
577            .push(Arc::new((restriction.clone(), narrower)));
578        Ok(())
579    }

581    /// A worker's existing snapshot may finish under the old generation;
582    /// future snapshots see this replacement and all permanent restrictions.
583    pub(crate) fn tighten(
584        &mut self,
585        restriction: &Theory,
586        generation: u64,
587        budget: &mut Budget<'_>,
588    ) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
589        budget.cancellation.poll()?;
590        if !self.started {
591            self.synchronize_budget(budget.statistics)?;
592        }
593        let bound = Bound::prepare(restriction, generation)?;
594        let charged = self.shared.budget.charge(bound.work());
595        self.account(budget);
596        charged?;
597        let work = self
598            .statistics
599            .counts
600            .work
601            .checked_add(bound.work())
602            .ok_or(Incomplete::CounterOverflow)?;
603        self.statistics.counts.work = work;
604        budget.cancellation.poll()?;
605        let previous = self
606            .shared
607            .restrictions
608            .write()
609            .unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner)
610            .bound
611            .replace(bound);
612        // Release retired storage outside the readers' snapshot lock.
613        drop(previous);
614        Ok(())
615    }

645    /// model the workers sent has been taken, and on every call after that.
646    pub(crate) fn propose(
647        &mut self,
648        certificate: Option<&Arc<Certification>>,
649        timed: bool,
650        budget: &mut Budget<'_>,
651    ) -> Result<Option<Interpretation>, Incomplete> {
652        if self.exhausted {
653            return Ok(None);
654        }
655        if !self.started {
656            self.synchronize_budget(budget.statistics)?;
657            if let Err(error) = self.start(certificate, timed) {
658                self.account(budget);
659                return Err(error);
660            }
661        }
662        loop {
663            budget.cancellation.poll()?;
664            match self
665                .receiver
666                .as_ref()
667                .ok_or(Incomplete::ClosedEnumerator)?
668                .recv_timeout(IDLE_WAIT)
669            {
670                Ok(model) => {
671                    self.account(budget);
672                    return Ok(Some(model));
673                }
674                Err(std::sync::mpsc::RecvTimeoutError::Timeout) => {}
675                Err(std::sync::mpsc::RecvTimeoutError::Disconnected) => {
676                    // Every worker has finished: the channel holds nothing
677                    // more, so the stop, if one was raised, follows every
678                    // model the workers verified.
679                    let joined = self.join();
680                    self.account(budget);
681                    if let Some(error) = self.shared.stopped() {
682                        return Err(error);
683                    }
684                    joined?;
685                    // Clean completion with no stop: every region created was
686                    // resolved, so the frontier counter is exactly zero. This
687                    // guards the accounting-regression class (a missing or
688                    // doubled decrement, or a total premature exit that abandons
689                    // regions) that the exact-family check cannot see.
690                    debug_assert_eq!(
691                        self.shared.termination.outstanding.load(Ordering::Acquire),
692                        0,
693                        "clean completion left regions outstanding"
694                    );
695                    self.exhausted = true;
696                    return Ok(None);
697                }
698            }
699        }
700    }

702    /// Before workers own leases, the coordinator's counters include every
703    /// setup charge, including certificate configuration since construction.
704    /// Reconcile them before another restriction or the first worker starts.
705    fn synchronize_budget(&mut self, statistics: SearchStatistics) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
706        let shared = Arc::get_mut(&mut self.shared).ok_or(Incomplete::InvalidWitness)?;
707        shared.budget = SharedBudget::new(shared.limits.search, statistics);
708        Ok(())
709    }

711    fn start(
712        &mut self,
713        certificate: Option<&Arc<Certification>>,
714        timed: bool,
715    ) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
716        self.start_with(certificate, timed, |shared, index, sender| {
717            std::thread::Builder::new()
718                .name("zetesis-region".into())
719                .spawn(move || contain_worker(&shared, || worker(&shared, index, &sender)))
720        })
721    }
722
723    /// Thread creation is an execution boundary: a refused launch closes and
724    /// joins the already launched subset before the incomplete result escapes.
725    fn start_with(
726        &mut self,
727        certificate: Option<&Arc<Certification>>,
728        timed: bool,
729        spawn: impl FnMut(
730            Arc<Shared>,
731            usize,
732            SyncSender<Interpretation>,
733        ) -> std::io::Result<JoinHandle<Option<WorkerReport>>>,
734    ) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
735        // The certificate is shared read-only; the Arc is cloned into the
736        // shared structure before any worker starts.
737        let shared = Arc::get_mut(&mut self.shared).ok_or(Incomplete::InvalidWitness)?;
738        shared.certificate = certificate.map(Arc::clone);
739        shared.timed = timed;
740        if let Err(error) = self.launch(spawn) {
741            self.shared.stop(error);
742            return self.stop().and(Err(error));
743        }
744        self.started = true;
745        Ok(())
746    }
747
748    fn launch(
749        &mut self,
750        mut spawn: impl FnMut(
751            Arc<Shared>,
752            usize,
753            SyncSender<Interpretation>,
754        ) -> std::io::Result<JoinHandle<Option<WorkerReport>>>,
755    ) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
756        self.handles
757            .try_reserve(self.shared.workers)
758            .map_err(|_| Incomplete::Allocation)?;
759        let sender = self.sender.take().ok_or(Incomplete::ClosedEnumerator)?;
760        for index in 0..self.shared.workers {
761            let handle = spawn(Arc::clone(&self.shared), index, sender.clone())
762                .map_err(|_| Incomplete::Allocation)?;
763            self.handles.push(handle);
764        }
765        Ok(())
766    }
767
768    /// Wait for every worker and merge its membership receipts; its region
769    /// receipts are already in the live counters.
770    ///
771    /// # Errors
772    /// A merged count beyond its width is the counter refusal.
773    fn join(&mut self) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
774        let mut failure = None;
775        for handle in self.handles.drain(..) {
776            let result = match handle.join() {
777                Ok(Some(report)) => merge_membership(&mut self.merged, &report.statistics),
778                Ok(None) => Ok(()),
779                Err(_) => Err(Incomplete::WorkerPanicked),
780            };
781            if let Err(error) = result {
782                self.shared.stop(error);
783                failure.get_or_insert(error);
784            }
785        }
786        // No worker can still own or publish work. Release abandoned payloads
787        // and high-water slot capacity even if the caller keeps the enumerator.
788        for index in 0..self.shared.workers {
789            let pending = std::mem::take(&mut *self.shared.queue(index));
790            drop(pending);
791        }
792        failure.map_or(Ok(()), Err)
793    }
794
795    /// The shared allowance's spent work and decisions become the
796    /// enumeration's search counters.
797    fn account(&self, budget: &mut Budget<'_>) {
798        let mut spent = SearchStatistics::default();
799        self.shared.budget.snapshot(&mut spent);
800        budget.statistics.work = budget.statistics.work.max(spent.work);
801        budget.statistics.decisions = budget.statistics.decisions.max(spent.decisions);
802    }
803}

806fn reserve_regions(queue: &mut VecDeque<Entry>, additional: usize) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
807    queue
808        .try_reserve(additional)
809        .map_err(|_| Incomplete::Allocation)

812/// An unwinding worker cannot account for the frontier it held. Close the
813/// run immediately, waking idle peers, and preserve an incomplete outcome.
814fn contain_worker(shared: &Shared, run: impl FnOnce() -> WorkerReport) -> Option<WorkerReport> {
815    if let Ok(report) = std::panic::catch_unwind(std::panic::AssertUnwindSafe(run)) {
816        Some(report)
817    } else {
818        shared.stop(Incomplete::WorkerPanicked);
819        None
820    }
821}

829/// Merge a joined worker's certificate and reduct receipts; its candidate,
830/// query and countermodel counts reached the live counters as it went, and
831/// the coordinator counts the stable models it returns. A sum beyond its
832/// counter's width is the counter refusal.
833fn merge_membership(into: &mut Statistics, from: &Statistics) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
834    fn add(counter: &mut u64, value: u64) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
835        *counter = counter
836            .checked_add(value)
837            .ok_or(Incomplete::CounterOverflow)?;
838        Ok(())
839    }
840    add(&mut into.reduct.original_work, from.reduct.original_work)?;
841    if let Some(from) = from.certified.as_ref() {
842        let into = into.certified.get_or_insert_default();
843        add(&mut into.checks, from.checks)?;
844        add(&mut into.stable, from.stable)?;
845        add(&mut into.refuted, from.refuted)?;
846        add(&mut into.failed, from.failed)?;
847        add(&mut into.checking_work, from.checking_work)?;
848        into.positive_check_peak_bytes = match (
849            into.positive_check_peak_bytes,
850            from.positive_check_peak_bytes,
851        ) {
852            (Some(a), Some(b)) => Some(a.max(b)),
853            (a, b) => a.or(b),
854        };
855    }
856    into.reduct.regions.add(from.reduct.regions)
857}

859/// One worker's walk, until the run closes, a stop is raised, or the
860/// enumeration stops listening.
861fn worker(shared: &Shared, index: usize, sender: &SyncSender<Interpretation>) -> WorkerReport {
862    let mut report = WorkerReport {
863        regions: RegionCounts::default(),
864        statistics: Statistics {
865            // The certificate records its checks in these receipts.
866            certified: shared
867                .certificate
868                .as_ref()
869                .map(|_| super::certified::CertifiedStatistics::default()),
870            phase_timings: shared.timed.then(SearchPhaseTimings::default),
871            ..Statistics::default()
872        },
873    };
874    let mut filter = None;
875    let mut reported = SearchPhaseTimings::default();
876    let mut search = SearchStatistics::default();
877    let mut membership = crate::prepared_reduct::State::with_index(Arc::clone(&shared.index));
878    // One lease serves this worker's consecutive regions, so the shared ledger
879    // is locked about twice per grant rather than twice per region. It settles
880    // before this worker steals or waits for another region and before it sends
881    // a model; a reservation settles it first, and exit or unwinding drops it.
882    // No unused permit is held where this worker could block.
883    let mut quota = shared.budget.lease(&shared.cancellation);
884    loop {
885        // Honour a stop before taking any region, so a worker with a deep deque
886        // does not run its whole subtree on after a stop was raised.
887        if shared.termination.is_closed() {
888            break;
889        }
890        let local = shared.take_local(index);
891        if local.is_none() {
892            quota.settle();
893        }
894        let Some(entry) = local.or_else(|| find_work(shared, index, IDLE_WAIT)) else {
895            break;
896        };
897        let stepped = {
898            let mut budget = Budget {
899                quota,
900                limits: shared.limits.search,
901                cancellation: &shared.cancellation,
902                statistics: search,
903            };
904            let result = step(
905                shared,
906                entry,
907                index,
908                &mut budget,
909                &mut membership,
910                &mut report,
911                &mut filter,
912            );
913            search = budget.statistics;
914            quota = budget.quota;
915            result
916        };
917        if let Some(measured) = report.statistics.phase_timings {
918            shared.live.add_timings(&reported, &measured);
919            reported = measured;
920        }
921        match stepped {
922            // A split's children are on this worker's deque; `outstanding` was
923            // already incremented before they were pushed.
924            Ok(Stepped::Split) => {}
925            // A resolved region leaves the frontier: resolve once, here, so
926            // the count has exactly one structural decrement point.
927            Ok(Stepped::Resolved(model)) => {
928                shared.termination.resolve();
929                if let Some(model) = model {
930                    quota.settle();
931                    if sender.send(model).is_err() {
932                        shared.close();
933                        break;
934                    }
935                }
936            }
937            Err(error) => {
938                shared.stop(error);
939                break;
940            }
941        }
942    }
943    report
944}

946/// Steal a region from another worker, waiting while any remains. `None` when
947/// the whole frontier is resolved or a stop closed the run. `idle_wait` bounds
948/// how long an idle worker waits before it looks for a region again and polls
949/// cancellation; the end of the walk ends the wait at once.
950fn find_work(shared: &Shared, index: usize, idle_wait: Duration) -> Option<Entry> {
951    let mut idle_rounds = 0u32;
952    loop {
953        if shared.termination.is_closed() {
954            return None;
955        }
956        // Try peers in round-robin order, skipping a busy queue so one owner's
957        // growth cannot block stealing from other workers. No two locks overlap.
958        for offset in 1..shared.workers {
959            let mut queue = match shared.queues[(index + offset) % shared.workers].try_lock() {
960                Ok(queue) => queue,
961                Err(TryLockError::Poisoned(error)) => error.into_inner(),
962                Err(TryLockError::WouldBlock) => continue,
963            };
964            if let Some(entry) = queue.pop_front() {
965                return Some(entry);
966            }
967        }
968        // No steal succeeded. Even with busy queues, zero means every created
969        // region was resolved; a split counts its children before publishing.
970        if shared.termination.is_resolved() {
971            return None;
972        }
973        // Busy queues and empty queues share the backoff, so lock contention
974        // cannot postpone cooperative control checks indefinitely.
975        idle_rounds += 1;
976        if idle_rounds < SPIN_ROUNDS_BEFORE_WAIT {
977            std::thread::yield_now();
978        } else {
979            shared.termination.wait_idle(idle_wait);
980            if let Err(error) = shared.cancellation.poll() {
981                shared.stop(error.into());
982                return None;
983            }
984            idle_rounds = 0;
985        }
986    }
987}

989/// What one `step` did to its region: split it into two children (now on the
990/// worker's deque), or resolved it — refuted, or a leaf with an optional
991/// verified model. The caller decrements `outstanding` once, on `Resolved`.
992enum Stepped {
993    Split,
994    Resolved(Option<Interpretation>),
995}
996
997/// Narrow one region and act on it: refuted, split, or a leaf decided by
998/// the reduct. A split publishes both children onto this worker's deque.
999fn step<'a>(
1000    shared: &'a Shared,
1001    (mut region, mut knowledge): Entry,
1002    index: usize,
1003    budget: &mut Budget<'a, WorkLease<'a>>,
1004    membership: &mut crate::prepared_reduct::State,
1005    report: &mut WorkerReport,
1006    filter: &mut Option<crate::region_filter::Worker<'a>>,
1007) -> Result<Stepped, Incomplete> {
1008    // The restrictions current when the region is taken, held for its
1009    // narrowing without the lock.
1010    let restrictions = shared
1011        .restrictions
1012        .read()
1013        .unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner)
1014        .clone();
1015    report.regions.regions += 1;
1016    Live::add(&shared.live.regions, 1);
1017    let before = report.regions;
1018    let started = timing::start(report.statistics.phase_timings.as_ref());
1019    let narrowing: Result<Narrowing, Incomplete> = (|| {
1020        let narrowing = super::regions::narrow(
1021            (shared.index.theory(), shared.index.narrower()),
1022            shared.producers.as_ref(),
1023            &restrictions,
1024            &mut region,
1025            &mut knowledge,
1026            budget,
1027            &mut report.regions,
1028        )?;
1029        if narrowing != Narrowing::Refuted
1030            && let Some(factory) = shared.filter.as_ref()
1031            && factory.check(
1032                filter,
1033                shared.index.theory(),
1034                &region,
1035                &shared.cancellation,
1036                &mut report.statistics.phase_timings,
1037            )? == crate::RegionFeasibility::Refuted
1038        {
1039            return Ok(Narrowing::Refuted);
1040        }
1041        Ok(narrowing)
1042    })();
1043    timing::finish(
1044        &mut report.statistics.phase_timings,
1045        Phase::Candidates,
1046        started,
1047    );
1048    let after = report.regions;
1049    Live::add(
1050        &shared.live.propagations,
1051        after.propagations - before.propagations,
1052    );
1053    Live::add(&shared.live.held, after.held - before.held);
1054    Live::add(&shared.live.cut, after.cut - before.cut);
1055    Live::add(&shared.live.work, after.work - before.work);
1056    let narrowing = narrowing?;
1057    if narrowing == Narrowing::Refuted {
1058        report.regions.refuted += 1;
1059        Live::add(&shared.live.refuted, 1);
1060        return Ok(Stepped::Resolved(None));
1061    }
1062    let Some(atom) = region.split_atom() else {
1063        report.regions.leaves += 1;
1064        Live::add(&shared.live.leaves, 1);
1065        return leaf(shared, &region, budget, membership, report).map(Stepped::Resolved);
1066    };
1067    budget.decide()?;
1068    // Each child carries its own copy of the knowledge, linear in the theory:
1069    // what the parent learned holds in both.
1070    let (cut, held) = region.split(atom);
1071    let held = (held, knowledge.clone());
1072    shared.publish_split(index, held, (cut, knowledge), reserve_regions)?;
1073    Ok(Stepped::Split)
1074}

1076/// Decide a leaf as the scalar proposer does.
1077fn leaf<'a>(
1078    shared: &Shared,
1079    region: &Region,
1080    budget: &mut Budget<'a, WorkLease<'a>>,
1081    membership: &mut crate::prepared_reduct::State,
1082    report: &mut WorkerReport,
1083) -> Result<Option<Interpretation>, Incomplete> {
1084    let candidate = super::regions::leaf_interpretation(shared.index.theory(), region)?;
1085    shared
1086        .candidates
1087        .fetch_update(Ordering::Relaxed, Ordering::Relaxed, |count| {
1088            (count < shared.limits.max_candidates).then(|| count + 1)
1089        })
1090        .map_err(|_| Incomplete::CandidateLimit)?;
1091    report.statistics.candidates += 1;
1092    let queries_before = report.statistics.countermodel_queries;
1093    let countermodels_before = report.statistics.countermodels;
1094    let decision: Result<Decision, Incomplete> = if let Some(certificate) = &shared.certificate {
1095        let mut search = budget.statistics;
1096        let wanted = certificate
1097            .checking_work_bound()?
1098            .min(shared.limits.max_verification_work);
1099        let reservation = budget.quota.reserve(wanted)?;
1100        let allowance = reservation.allowance();
1101        let mut limits = shared.limits;
1102        limits.max_verification_work = allowance;
1103        if allowance < wanted {
1104            // Only a shortage of global work lowers the search ceiling.
1105            // A complete reservation preserves a stricter verification
1106            // ceiling's own error attribution.
1107            limits.search.max_work = search
1108                .work
1109                .checked_add(allowance)
1110                .ok_or(Incomplete::CounterOverflow)?;
1111        }
1112        let verdict = certified::classify(
1113            certificate,
1114            &candidate,
1115            limits,
1116            budget.cancellation,
1117            &mut report.statistics,
1118            &mut search,
1119        );
1120        reservation.finish(search.work - budget.statistics.work)?;
1121        budget.statistics = search;
1122        verdict.map(Decision::from)
1123    } else {
1124        membership
1125            .check(
1126                shared.index.theory(),
1127                &candidate,
1128                shared.limits,
1129                budget,
1130                &mut report.statistics,
1131            )
1132            .map(Decision::from)
1133    };
1134    // Query entry is a receipt even when membership checking stops before
1135    // producing a verdict. Publish it before propagating that failure.
1136    Live::add(
1137        &shared.live.countermodel_queries,
1138        report.statistics.countermodel_queries - queries_before,
1139    );
1140    Live::add(
1141        &shared.live.countermodels,
1142        report.statistics.countermodels - countermodels_before,
1143    );
1144    match decision? {
1145        Decision::Stable => Ok(Some(candidate)),
1146        Decision::Refuted => Ok(None),
1147        Decision::Invalid => Err(Incomplete::InvalidWitness),
1148    }
1149}
