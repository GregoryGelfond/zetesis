1//! Regions walked by several workers at once, sharing immutable preparation,
2//! a cumulative allowance and a work-stealing frontier of regions still to visit.
3//!
4//! Each worker owns a work-stealing deque of regions with their knowledge, a
5//! lease on the enumeration's shared allowance that serves its consecutive
6//! regions, and its own evaluation workspace. The enumeration builds the
7//! original-theory index before the first proposal and hands each worker a
8//! clone when it is spawned; candidate and reduct traversals share that
9//! authenticated index but never their mutable knowledge. A worker pops a
10//! region from its own deque, narrows it from the knowledge it carries, drops
11//! it when refuted, splits it otherwise and pushes both children back onto its
12//! deque; a worker whose deque is empty steals a
13//! region from a peer. At a leaf it decides membership as the scalar proposer
14//! does, by the class certificate when one applies and else by the
15//! proper-subset query as a region tree; a stable model is sent to the
16//! enumeration. The regions partition the candidate space exactly
17//! (`Cube.split_partition`, `Cube.split_disjoint` of `Search.lean`), so no leaf
18//! is visited by two workers and every leaf by one, whatever the interleaving;
19//! the order in which models arrive is the schedule's and is not a property of
20//! the result, and no two runs promise the same order.
21//!
22//! A restriction added while the workers run narrows the regions not yet
23//! visited: each worker reads the restrictions before a narrowing, and a
24//! region reached before a restriction existed gets fresh knowledge under
25//! it. A work, decision or candidate ceiling is shared: the first worker
26//! to exhaust it raises the stop, the others stop at their next charge or
27//! their next region, and the enumeration is incomplete. The models the
28//! workers verified before they stopped are delivered first and the stop
29//! after them, so a leaf admitted under the candidate ceiling is never
30//! lost to a worker the ceiling refused. A cancellation observed by the
31//! coordinator can end its pull immediately; explicit stop or drop joins any
32//! remaining workers. The region counts and reading
33//! work, the candidates, the countermodel counts and the phase timings are
34//! the workers' live counters, current while they run; the certificate and
35//! reduct receipts are merged when the workers have finished, so a snapshot
36//! taken earlier lacks them. Phase timings, when enabled, are the workers'
37//! own narrowing and leaf decisions summed over the workers, so they may
38//! exceed the wall time of the walk.
39//!
40//! Each deque has its own mutex: local removal locks only that deque, a split
41//! publication locks that deque and then, after releasing it, the idle gate to
42//! wake one idle worker, and a thief skips a busy peer. Narrowing, payload cloning, reduct
43//! checks and model sends hold no queue lock. Slot growth is fallible and occurs
44//! before either child is published. An atomic counter of unresolved regions
45//! carries termination — a split raises it before pushing the children, a
46//! resolved region lowers it once, and an unsuccessful thief stops only once
47//! that counter has reached zero. An atomic closed flag, set on the first stop
48//! or when the enumeration stops listening, halts the others at their next region.
49//! Idle workers wait at a gate that the last resolution and every close open, and
50//! that a publication opens for one waiter, so none sleeps out its timed wait
51//! after the walk has ended or while a region it could take is published. The
52//! timed wait bounds how late an idle worker sees a cancellation, and how late it
53//! finds a region left in a deque that was busy when it last looked.
54//!
55//! A local depth-first walk keeps at most one older sibling per ancestor and
56//! the two newest children. Each split decides another atom, and a worker steals
57//! only with an empty deque, so each deque holds at most `atom_count + 1` entries.
58//! Storage grows with the observed depth rather than reserving that bound for
59//! every worker. Capacity and payloads are released after joining. Fallible slot
60//! growth does not make region or knowledge cloning fallible.

123/// What ends the walk — every region resolved, or a close — and the gate idle
124/// workers wait at for either.
125///
126/// The count and the flag are atomics because the region loop reads them for
127/// every region; the gate guards the number of idle waiters. Every transition
128/// that ends the walk changes its atomic first and then takes the gate to wake
129/// the waiters, and an idle worker takes the gate and re-checks both conditions,
130/// and the peers' deques, before it waits. Whichever reaches the gate first, no
131/// idle worker waits past the end of the walk: a worker that takes the gate after
132/// the ending transition sees it, and one that took the gate before is already
133/// waiting when the wake comes. A publication pushes its children, releases its
134/// deque and then takes the gate to wake one waiter, so by the same argument no
135/// waiter misses a region published after its re-check. Zero is terminal, since
136/// a region in hand counts until it is resolved. Every wait stays bounded by its
137/// timeout, so correctness never rests on a wake: the timeout is how an idle
138/// worker sees a cancellation, or a region left in a deque its re-check found
139/// busy.
140struct Termination {
141    /// Created-but-unresolved regions across every worker's deque and hand: a
142    /// split adds one (before pushing its children), a refuted or decided
143    /// region subtracts one. It starts at one for the root and reaches zero
144    /// exactly when the whole frontier is resolved; a read of zero is a true
145    /// zero, so a worker that finds every deque empty and reads zero is done.
146    outstanding: AtomicUsize,
147    /// A stop was raised (error or cancellation) or the enumeration stopped
148    /// listening; workers check it before each region and while idle, and
149    /// exit. Normal termination is `outstanding` reaching zero.
150    closed: AtomicBool,
151    /// The number of idle workers waiting on `idle`. Held to re-check the
152    /// walk's end and the peers' deques before an idle wait, to wake one waiter
153    /// after a publication, and to wake every waiter after the walk ends.
154    gate: Mutex<usize>,
155    /// Idle workers wait here for a published region or the end of the walk.
156    idle: Condvar,
157}
158
159impl Termination {
160    /// The root is the one outstanding region until it is split.
161    fn new() -> Self {
162        Self {
163            outstanding: AtomicUsize::new(1),
164            closed: AtomicBool::new(false),
165            gate: Mutex::new(0),
166            idle: Condvar::new(),
167        }
168    }
169
170    /// A split replaces its parent by two children: count the net gain of one
171    /// before either child becomes visible.
172    fn grow(&self) {
173        self.outstanding.fetch_add(1, Ordering::AcqRel);
174    }
175
176    /// A refuted or decided region leaves the frontier. The last one ends the
177    /// walk and wakes every idle worker.
178    fn resolve(&self) {
179        let previous = self.outstanding.fetch_sub(1, Ordering::AcqRel);
180        debug_assert_ne!(previous, 0, "a region was resolved twice");
181        if previous == 1 {
182            self.wake_idle();
183        }
184    }
185
186    /// Close the walk: workers take no further region, and idle workers wake.
187    fn close(&self) {
188        self.closed.store(true, Ordering::Release);
189        self.wake_idle();
190    }
191
192    fn is_resolved(&self) -> bool {
193        self.outstanding.load(Ordering::Acquire) == 0
194    }
195
196    fn is_closed(&self) -> bool {
197        self.closed.load(Ordering::Acquire)
198    }
199
200    fn has_ended(&self) -> bool {
201        self.is_closed() || self.is_resolved()
202    }
203
204    /// Wake one idle worker, if any waits, after a publication.
205    fn wake_one(&self) {
206        let waiting = self.gate.lock().unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner);
207        if *waiting != 0 {
208            self.idle.notify_one();
209        }
210    }
211
212    /// Wake every idle worker, after the transition that ended the walk.
213    fn wake_idle(&self) {
214        let _gate = self.gate.lock().unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner);
215        self.idle.notify_all();
216    }
217}

235/// One phase's calls and elapsed nanoseconds, summed over the workers.
236#[derive(Default)]
237struct LivePhase {
238    calls: AtomicU64,
239    nanos: AtomicU64,
240    /// Sticky measurement incompleteness, independent of semantic coverage.
241    overflowed: AtomicBool,
242}
243
244impl LivePhase {
245    /// Keep each counter monotone, saturating only after marking the
246    /// measurement incomplete. Live snapshots read independent atomics;
247    /// after workers join, the totals are exact or explicitly overflowed.
248    fn add(&self, counter: &AtomicU64, amount: u64) {
249        if amount == 0 {
250            return;
251        }
252        if counter
253            .fetch_update(Ordering::Relaxed, Ordering::Relaxed, |value| {
254                value.checked_add(amount)
255            })
256            .is_err()
257        {
258            self.overflowed.store(true, Ordering::Relaxed);
259            // Every writer only increases this counter. An overflow makes
260            // MAX absorbing, including concurrent successful additions.
261            counter.store(u64::MAX, Ordering::Relaxed);
262        }
263    }
264}
265
266impl Live {
267    fn add(counter: &AtomicU64, amount: u64) {
268        counter.fetch_add(amount, Ordering::Relaxed);
269    }
270    fn read(counter: &AtomicU64) -> u64 {
271        counter.load(Ordering::Relaxed)
272    }
273
274    /// Add what a worker measured since its last report.
275    fn add_timings(&self, before: &SearchPhaseTimings, after: &SearchPhaseTimings) {
276        for (live, (before, after)) in self.timings.iter().zip(phases(before).zip(phases(after))) {
277            // A worker can become incomplete without changing its retained
278            // numeric prefix, so propagate its flag even for a zero delta.
279            if before.overflowed || after.overflowed {
280                live.overflowed.store(true, Ordering::Relaxed);
281            }
282            let calls = after.calls.saturating_sub(before.calls);
283            let elapsed = after.elapsed.saturating_sub(before.elapsed);
284            let nanos = u64::try_from(elapsed.as_nanos()).unwrap_or_else(|_| {
285                live.overflowed.store(true, Ordering::Relaxed);
286                u64::MAX
287            });
288            live.add(&live.calls, calls);
289            live.add(&live.nanos, nanos);
290        }
291    }
292
293    fn timings(&self) -> SearchPhaseTimings {
294        let read = |live: &LivePhase| PhaseMeasurement {
295            calls: Self::read(&live.calls),
296            elapsed: Duration::from_nanos(Self::read(&live.nanos)),
297            overflowed: live.overflowed.load(Ordering::Relaxed),
298        };
299        let [
300            candidates,
301            original_validation,
302            reduct,
303            reduct_preparation,
304            certified,
305        ] = &self.timings;
306        SearchPhaseTimings {
307            candidates: read(candidates),
308            original_validation: read(original_validation),
309            reduct: read(reduct),
310            reduct_preparation: read(reduct_preparation),
311            certified: read(certified),
312        }
313    }
314}

328impl Shared {
329    fn queue(&self, index: usize) -> MutexGuard<'_, VecDeque<Entry>> {
330        self.queues[index]
331            .lock()
332            .unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner)
333    }
334
335    fn take_local(&self, index: usize) -> Option<Entry> {
336        self.queue(index).pop_back()
337    }
338
339    /// Take the oldest region of a peer's deque, trying peers in round-robin
340    /// order and skipping a busy one, so one owner's growth cannot block
341    /// stealing from the others. No two queue locks overlap, and none is
342    /// waited for.
343    fn steal(&self, index: usize) -> Option<Entry> {
344        for offset in 1..self.workers {
345            let mut queue = match self.queues[(index + offset) % self.workers].try_lock() {
346                Ok(queue) => queue,
347                Err(TryLockError::Poisoned(error)) => error.into_inner(),
348                Err(TryLockError::WouldBlock) => continue,
349            };
350            if let Some(entry) = queue.pop_front() {
351                return Some(entry);
352            }
353        }
354        None
355    }
356
357    /// Wait at the gate for a published region or the end of the walk, for at
358    /// most `timeout`. Under the gate, the walk's end and the peers' deques are
359    /// re-checked first; a region found then is returned at once. `None` does
360    /// not mean the walk ended: the caller re-checks.
361    fn wait_for_work(&self, index: usize, timeout: Duration) -> Option<Entry> {
362        let mut waiting = self
363            .termination
364            .gate
365            .lock()
366            .unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner);
367        if self.termination.has_ended() {
368            return None;
369        }
370        if let Some(entry) = self.steal(index) {
371            return Some(entry);
372        }
373        *waiting += 1;
374        let (mut waiting, _) = self
375            .termination
376            .idle
377            .wait_timeout(waiting, timeout)
378            .unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner);
379        *waiting -= 1;
380        None
381    }
382
383    /// Prepare space for both children before the count or either queue entry
384    /// changes. The injected reservation is the sole fallible storage effect;
385    /// success must reserve `additional` slots, as `reserve_regions` does.
386    fn publish_split(
387        &self,
388        index: usize,
389        held: Entry,
390        cut: Entry,
391        reserve: impl FnOnce(&mut VecDeque<Entry>, usize) -> Result<(), Incomplete>,
392    ) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
393        let mut queue = self.queue(index);
394        reserve(&mut queue, 2)?;
395        // The parent is still outstanding. Count its net gain before making
396        // either child visible; the reserved pushes cannot allocate.
397        self.termination.grow();
398        queue.push_back(held);
399        queue.push_back(cut);
400        // Release the deque before taking the gate: a waiter holds the gate
401        // while it tries the deques, and never waits for a queue lock.
402        drop(queue);
403        self.termination.wake_one();
404        Ok(())
405    }
406
407    /// Close for a normal end of work (no error): workers exit when they next
408    /// check, idle ones at once, without recording a stop.
409    fn close(&self) {
410        self.termination.close();
411    }
412
413    /// Raise a stop: work closes, and the first stop is the one reported.
414    fn stop(&self, error: Incomplete) {
415        self.stopped
416            .lock()
417            .unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner)
418            .get_or_insert(error);
419        self.termination.close();
420    }
421
422    /// The first stop raised, if any.
423    fn stopped(&self) -> Option<Incomplete> {
424        *self.stopped.lock().unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner)
425    }
426}

460impl ParallelRegions {
461    /// Prepare the shared structure, queueing the root region with no
462    /// knowledge; its first narrowing creates each knowledge slot. The
463    /// workers start on the first proposal, once the enumeration's
464    /// certificates are configured and its original index is built.
465    pub(crate) fn new(
466        theory: &Theory,
467        workers: NonZeroUsize,
468        limits: Limits,
469        cancellation: Cancellation,
470        budget: &mut Budget<'_>,
471    ) -> Result<Self, Incomplete> {
472        let super::regions::Opened {
473            producers,
474            statistics,
475        } = super::regions::open(theory, budget)?;
476        let (sender, receiver) = sync_channel(workers.get() * CHANNEL_SLACK);
477        let root = (
478            Region::all_open(theory.atom_count()),
479            CandidateKnowledge::default(),
480        );
481        let mut queues = crate::search::storage(workers.get())?;
482        let mut first = VecDeque::new();
483        reserve_regions(&mut first, 1)?;
484        first.push_back(root);
485        queues.push(Mutex::new(first));
486        for _ in 1..workers.get() {
487            queues.push(Mutex::new(VecDeque::new()));
488        }
489        Ok(Self {
490            shared: Arc::new(Shared {
491                producers,
492                certificate: None,
493                filter: None,
494                restrictions: RwLock::new(Conditions::default()),
495                queues,
496                termination: Termination::new(),
497                stopped: Mutex::new(None),
498                budget: SharedBudget::new(limits.search, budget.statistics),
499                limits,
500                cancellation,
501                workers: workers.get(),
502                timed: false,
503                candidates: AtomicU64::new(0),
504                live: Live::default(),
505            }),
506            receiver: Some(receiver),
507            sender: Some(sender),
508            handles: Vec::new(),
509            started: false,
510            exhausted: false,
511            statistics,
512            merged: Statistics::default(),
513            scratch: NarrowingScratch::default(),
514        })
515    }

607    /// Restrict every region not yet visited to the classical models of
608    /// `restriction`; the workers read it before their next narrowing.
609    /// Indexing it is charged to the shared allowance the workers draw on,
610    /// and the enumeration's counters take the charge at once.
611    pub(crate) fn restrict(
612        &mut self,
613        restriction: &Theory,
614        budget: &mut Budget<'_>,
615    ) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
616        if !self.started {
617            self.synchronize_budget(budget.statistics)?;
618        }
619        let narrower = Narrower::try_new(restriction).map_err(super::regions::stopped)?;
620        self.shared.budget.charge(narrower.work())?;
621        self.account(budget);
622        self.statistics.counts.work += narrower.work();
623        let mut restrictions = self
624            .shared
625            .restrictions
626            .write()
627            .unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner);
628        restrictions
629            .permanent
630            .try_reserve(1)
631            .map_err(|_| Incomplete::Allocation)?;
632        restrictions
633            .permanent
634            .push(Arc::new((restriction.clone(), narrower)));
635        Ok(())
636    }

638    /// A worker's existing snapshot may finish under the old generation;
639    /// future snapshots see this replacement and all permanent restrictions.
640    pub(crate) fn tighten(
641        &mut self,
642        restriction: &Theory,
643        generation: u64,
644        budget: &mut Budget<'_>,
645    ) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
646        budget.cancellation.poll()?;
647        if !self.started {
648            self.synchronize_budget(budget.statistics)?;
649        }
650        let bound = Bound::prepare(restriction, generation)?;
651        let charged = self.shared.budget.charge(bound.work());
652        self.account(budget);
653        charged?;
654        let work = self
655            .statistics
656            .counts
657            .work
658            .checked_add(bound.work())
659            .ok_or(Incomplete::CounterOverflow)?;
660        self.statistics.counts.work = work;
661        budget.cancellation.poll()?;
662        let previous = self
663            .shared
664            .restrictions
665            .write()
666            .unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner)
667            .bound
668            .replace(bound);
669        // Release retired storage outside the readers' snapshot lock.
670        drop(previous);
671        Ok(())
672    }

674    pub(super) fn permits_positive(
675        &mut self,
676        candidate: &Interpretation,
677        budget: &mut Budget<'_>,
678        timings: &mut Option<crate::SearchPhaseTimings>,
679    ) -> Result<bool, Incomplete> {
680        if self.started {
681            return Err(Incomplete::LateCertificate);
682        }
683        let restrictions = self
684            .shared
685            .restrictions
686            .read()
687            .unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner);
688        super::regions::permits(
689            &restrictions,
690            self.shared.filter.as_ref(),
691            candidate,
692            &mut self.scratch,
693            budget,
694            &mut self.statistics.counts,
695            timings,
696        )
697    }

699    /// The next verified stable model, or `None` once the workers have
700    /// covered the root. Starts the workers on the first call, handing each
701    /// a clone of `index`, the enumeration's one original index, with the
702    /// certificate the enumeration holds at that moment, timing their
703    /// phases when `timed`. A stop a worker raised is returned once every
704    /// model the workers sent has been taken, and on every call after that.
705    pub(crate) fn propose(
706        &mut self,
707        index: &Arc<IndexedTheory>,
708        certificate: Option<&Arc<Certification>>,
709        timed: bool,
710        budget: &mut Budget<'_>,
711    ) -> Result<Option<Interpretation>, Incomplete> {
712        if self.exhausted {
713            return Ok(None);
714        }
715        if !self.started {
716            self.synchronize_budget(budget.statistics)?;
717            if let Err(error) = self.start(index, certificate, timed) {
718                self.account(budget);
719                return Err(error);
720            }
721        }
722        loop {
723            budget.cancellation.poll()?;
724            match self
725                .receiver
726                .as_ref()
727                .ok_or(Incomplete::ClosedEnumerator)?
728                .recv_timeout(IDLE_WAIT)
729            {
730                Ok(model) => {
731                    self.account(budget);
732                    return Ok(Some(model));
733                }
734                Err(std::sync::mpsc::RecvTimeoutError::Timeout) => {}
735                Err(std::sync::mpsc::RecvTimeoutError::Disconnected) => {
736                    // Every worker has finished: the channel holds nothing
737                    // more, so the stop, if one was raised, follows every
738                    // model the workers verified.
739                    let joined = self.join();
740                    self.account(budget);
741                    if let Some(error) = self.shared.stopped() {
742                        return Err(error);
743                    }
744                    joined?;
745                    // Clean completion with no stop: every region created was
746                    // resolved, so the frontier counter is exactly zero. This
747                    // guards the accounting-regression class (a missing or
748                    // doubled decrement, or a total premature exit that abandons
749                    // regions) that the exact-family check cannot see.
750                    debug_assert_eq!(
751                        self.shared.termination.outstanding.load(Ordering::Acquire),
752                        0,
753                        "clean completion left regions outstanding"
754                    );
755                    self.exhausted = true;
756                    return Ok(None);
757                }
758            }
759        }
760    }

762    /// Before workers own leases, the coordinator's counters include every
763    /// setup charge, including certificate configuration since construction.
764    /// Reconcile them before another restriction or the first worker starts.
765    fn synchronize_budget(&mut self, statistics: SearchStatistics) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
766        let shared = Arc::get_mut(&mut self.shared).ok_or(Incomplete::InvalidWitness)?;
767        shared.budget = SharedBudget::new(shared.limits.search, statistics);
768        Ok(())

771    fn start(
772        &mut self,
773        index: &Arc<IndexedTheory>,
774        certificate: Option<&Arc<Certification>>,
775        timed: bool,
776    ) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
777        self.start_with(certificate, timed, |shared, slot, sender| {
778            let original = Arc::clone(index);
779            std::thread::Builder::new()
780                .name("zetesis-region".into())
781                .spawn(move || {
782                    contain_worker(&shared, || worker(&shared, &original, slot, &sender))
783                })
784        })
785    }

787    /// Thread creation is an execution boundary: a refused launch closes and
788    /// joins the already launched subset before the incomplete result escapes.
789    fn start_with(
790        &mut self,
791        certificate: Option<&Arc<Certification>>,
792        timed: bool,
793        spawn: impl FnMut(
794            Arc<Shared>,
795            usize,
796            SyncSender<Interpretation>,
797        ) -> std::io::Result<JoinHandle<Option<WorkerReport>>>,
798    ) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
799        // The certificate is shared read-only; the Arc is cloned into the
800        // shared structure before any worker starts.
801        let shared = Arc::get_mut(&mut self.shared).ok_or(Incomplete::InvalidWitness)?;
802        shared.certificate = certificate.map(Arc::clone);
803        shared.timed = timed;
804        if let Err(error) = self.launch(spawn) {
805            self.shared.stop(error);
806            return self.stop().and(Err(error));
807        }
808        self.started = true;
809        Ok(())
810    }
811
812    fn launch(
813        &mut self,
814        mut spawn: impl FnMut(
815            Arc<Shared>,
816            usize,
817            SyncSender<Interpretation>,
818        ) -> std::io::Result<JoinHandle<Option<WorkerReport>>>,
819    ) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
820        self.handles
821            .try_reserve(self.shared.workers)
822            .map_err(|_| Incomplete::Allocation)?;
823        let sender = self.sender.take().ok_or(Incomplete::ClosedEnumerator)?;
824        for index in 0..self.shared.workers {
825            let handle = spawn(Arc::clone(&self.shared), index, sender.clone())
826                .map_err(|_| Incomplete::Allocation)?;
827            self.handles.push(handle);
828        }
829        Ok(())
830    }

832    /// Wait for every worker and merge its membership receipts; its region
833    /// receipts are already in the live counters.
834    ///
835    /// # Errors
836    /// A merged count beyond its width is the counter refusal.
837    fn join(&mut self) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
838        let mut failure = None;
839        for handle in self.handles.drain(..) {
840            let result = match handle.join() {
841                Ok(Some(report)) => merge_membership(&mut self.merged, &report.statistics),
842                Ok(None) => Ok(()),
843                Err(_) => Err(Incomplete::WorkerPanicked),
844            };
845            if let Err(error) = result {
846                self.shared.stop(error);
847                failure.get_or_insert(error);
848            }
849        }
850        // No worker can still own or publish work. Release abandoned payloads
851        // and high-water slot capacity even if the caller keeps the enumerator.
852        for index in 0..self.shared.workers {
853            let pending = std::mem::take(&mut *self.shared.queue(index));
854            drop(pending);
855        }
856        failure.map_or(Ok(()), Err)
857    }

859    /// The shared allowance's spent work and decisions become the
860    /// enumeration's search counters.
861    fn account(&self, budget: &mut Budget<'_>) {
862        let mut spent = SearchStatistics::default();
863        self.shared.budget.snapshot(&mut spent);
864        budget.statistics.work = budget.statistics.work.max(spent.work);
865        budget.statistics.decisions = budget.statistics.decisions.max(spent.decisions);
866    }
867}

876/// An unwinding worker cannot account for the frontier it held. Close the
877/// run immediately, waking idle peers, and preserve an incomplete outcome.
878fn contain_worker(shared: &Shared, run: impl FnOnce() -> WorkerReport) -> Option<WorkerReport> {
879    if let Ok(report) = std::panic::catch_unwind(std::panic::AssertUnwindSafe(run)) {
880        Some(report)
881    } else {
882        shared.stop(Incomplete::WorkerPanicked);
883        None
884    }

893/// Merge a joined worker's certificate and reduct receipts; its candidate,
894/// query and countermodel counts reached the live counters as it went, and
895/// the coordinator counts the stable models it returns. A sum beyond its
896/// counter's width is the counter refusal.
897fn merge_membership(into: &mut Statistics, from: &Statistics) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
898    fn add(counter: &mut u64, value: u64) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
899        *counter = counter
900            .checked_add(value)
901            .ok_or(Incomplete::CounterOverflow)?;
902        Ok(())
903    }
904    add(&mut into.reduct.original_work, from.reduct.original_work)?;
905    if let Some(from) = from.certified.as_ref() {
906        let into = into.certified.get_or_insert_default();
907        add(&mut into.checks, from.checks)?;
908        add(&mut into.stable, from.stable)?;
909        add(&mut into.refuted, from.refuted)?;
910        add(&mut into.failed, from.failed)?;
911        add(&mut into.checking_work, from.checking_work)?;
912        into.positive_check_peak_bytes = match (
913            into.positive_check_peak_bytes,
914            from.positive_check_peak_bytes,
915        ) {
916            (Some(a), Some(b)) => Some(a.max(b)),
917            (a, b) => a.or(b),
918        };
919    }
920    into.reduct.regions.add(from.reduct.regions)
921}

923/// One worker's walk over `original`, the enumeration's index, until the run
924/// closes, a stop is raised, or the enumeration stops listening.
925fn worker(
926    shared: &Shared,
927    original: &IndexedTheory,
928    index: usize,
929    sender: &SyncSender<Interpretation>,
930) -> WorkerReport {
931    let mut report = WorkerReport {
932        regions: RegionCounts::default(),
933        statistics: Statistics {
934            // The certificate records its checks in these receipts.
935            certified: shared
936                .certificate
937                .as_ref()
938                .map(|_| super::certified::CertifiedStatistics::default()),
939            phase_timings: shared.timed.then(SearchPhaseTimings::default),
940            ..Statistics::default()
941        },
942    };
943    let mut reported = SearchPhaseTimings::default();
944    let mut search = SearchStatistics::default();
945    let mut workspaces = Workspaces::new();
946    // One lease serves this worker's consecutive regions, so the shared ledger
947    // is locked about twice per grant rather than twice per region. It settles
948    // before this worker steals or waits for another region and before it sends
949    // a model; a reservation settles it first, and exit or unwinding drops it.
950    // No unused permit is held where this worker could block.
951    let mut quota = shared.budget.lease(&shared.cancellation);
952    loop {
953        // Honour a stop before taking any region, so a worker with a deep deque
954        // does not run its whole subtree on after a stop was raised.
955        if shared.termination.is_closed() {
956            break;
957        }
958        let local = shared.take_local(index);
959        if local.is_none() {
960            quota.settle();
961        }
962        let Some(entry) = local.or_else(|| find_work(shared, index, IDLE_WAIT)) else {
963            break;
964        };
965        let stepped = {
966            let mut budget = Budget {
967                quota,
968                limits: shared.limits.search,
969                cancellation: &shared.cancellation,
970                statistics: search,
971            };
972            let result = step(
973                shared,
974                original,
975                entry,
976                index,
977                &mut workspaces,
978                &mut budget,
979                &mut report,
980            );
981            search = budget.statistics;
982            quota = budget.quota;
983            result
984        };
985        if let Some(measured) = report.statistics.phase_timings {
986            shared.live.add_timings(&reported, &measured);
987            reported = measured;
988        }
989        match stepped {
990            // A split's children are on this worker's deque; `outstanding` was
991            // already incremented before they were pushed.
992            Ok(Stepped::Split) => {}
993            // A resolved region leaves the frontier: resolve once, here, so
994            // the count has exactly one structural decrement point.
995            Ok(Stepped::Resolved(model)) => {
996                shared.termination.resolve();
997                if let Some(model) = model {
998                    quota.settle();
999                    if sender.send(model).is_err() {
1000                        shared.close();
1001                        break;
1002                    }
1003                }
1004            }
1005            Err(error) => {
1006                shared.stop(error);
1007                break;
1008            }
1009        }
1010    }
1011    report
1012}

1014/// Steal a region from another worker, waiting while any remains. `None` when
1015/// the whole frontier is resolved or a stop closed the run. `idle_wait` bounds
1016/// how long an idle worker waits before it looks for a region again and polls
1017/// cancellation; a publication or the end of the walk ends the wait at once.
1018fn find_work(shared: &Shared, index: usize, idle_wait: Duration) -> Option<Entry> {
1019    let mut idle_rounds = 0u32;
1020    loop {
1021        if shared.termination.is_closed() {
1022            return None;
1023        }
1024        if let Some(entry) = shared.steal(index) {
1025            return Some(entry);
1026        }
1027        // No steal succeeded. Even with busy queues, zero means every created
1028        // region was resolved; a split counts its children before publishing.
1029        if shared.termination.is_resolved() {
1030            return None;
1031        }
1032        // Busy queues and empty queues share the backoff, so lock contention
1033        // cannot postpone cooperative control checks indefinitely.
1034        idle_rounds += 1;
1035        if idle_rounds < SPIN_ROUNDS_BEFORE_WAIT {
1036            std::thread::yield_now();
1037        } else {
1038            if let Some(entry) = shared.wait_for_work(index, idle_wait) {
1039                return Some(entry);
1040            }
1041            if let Err(error) = shared.cancellation.poll() {
1042                shared.stop(error.into());
1043                return None;
1044            }
1045            idle_rounds = 0;
1046        }
1047    }
1048}

1050/// What one `step` did to its region: split it into two children (now on the
1051/// worker's deque), or resolved it — refuted, or a leaf with an optional
1052/// verified model. The caller decrements `outstanding` once, on `Resolved`.
1053enum Stepped {
1054    Split,
1055    Resolved(Option<Interpretation>),
1056}

1058/// What one worker reuses across its regions: the narrowing worklists, the
1059/// reduct-membership state and the region filter's worker, made when first
1060/// needed.
1061struct Workspaces<'a> {
1062    scratch: NarrowingScratch,
1063    membership: crate::prepared_reduct::State,
1064    filter: Option<crate::region_filter::Worker<'a>>,
1065}
1066
1067impl Workspaces<'_> {
1068    fn new() -> Self {
1069        Self {
1070            scratch: NarrowingScratch::default(),
1071            membership: crate::prepared_reduct::State::new(crate::SearchMethod::Regions),
1072            filter: None,
1073        }
1074    }
1075}

1077/// Narrow one region and act on it: refuted, split, or a leaf decided by
1078/// the reduct. A split publishes both children onto this worker's deque.
1079fn step<'a>(
1080    shared: &'a Shared,
1081    original: &IndexedTheory,
1082    (mut region, mut knowledge): Entry,
1083    index: usize,
1084    workspaces: &mut Workspaces<'a>,
1085    budget: &mut Budget<'a, WorkLease<'a>>,
1086    report: &mut WorkerReport,
1087) -> Result<Stepped, Incomplete> {
1088    // The restrictions current when the region is taken, held for its
1089    // narrowing without the lock.
1090    let restrictions = shared
1091        .restrictions
1092        .read()
1093        .unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner)
1094        .clone();
1095    report.regions.regions += 1;
1096    Live::add(&shared.live.regions, 1);
1097    let before = report.regions;
1098    let started = timing::start(report.statistics.phase_timings.as_ref());
1099    let narrowing: Result<Narrowing, Incomplete> = (|| {
1100        let narrowing = super::regions::narrow(
1101            (
1102                original.theory(),
1103                original.narrower(),
1104                shared.producers.as_ref(),
1105            ),
1106            &restrictions,
1107            &mut region,
1108            &mut knowledge,
1109            &mut workspaces.scratch,
1110            budget,
1111            &mut report.regions,
1112        )?;
1113        if narrowing != Narrowing::Refuted
1114            && let Some(factory) = shared.filter.as_ref()
1115            && factory.check(
1116                &mut workspaces.filter,
1117                original.theory(),
1118                &region,
1119                &shared.cancellation,
1120                &mut report.statistics.phase_timings,
1121            )? == crate::RegionFeasibility::Refuted
1122        {
1123            return Ok(Narrowing::Refuted);
1124        }
1125        Ok(narrowing)
1126    })();
1127    timing::finish(
1128        &mut report.statistics.phase_timings,
1129        Phase::Candidates,
1130        started,
1131    );
1132    let after = report.regions;
1133    Live::add(
1134        &shared.live.propagations,
1135        after.propagations - before.propagations,
1136    );
1137    Live::add(&shared.live.held, after.held - before.held);
1138    Live::add(&shared.live.cut, after.cut - before.cut);
1139    Live::add(&shared.live.work, after.work - before.work);
1140    let narrowing = narrowing?;
1141    if narrowing == Narrowing::Refuted {
1142        report.regions.refuted += 1;
1143        Live::add(&shared.live.refuted, 1);
1144        return Ok(Stepped::Resolved(None));
1145    }
1146    let Some(atom) = region.split_atom() else {
1147        report.regions.leaves += 1;
1148        Live::add(&shared.live.leaves, 1);
1149        return leaf(
1150            shared,
1151            original,
1152            &region,
1153            budget,
1154            &mut workspaces.membership,
1155            report,
1156        )
1157        .map(Stepped::Resolved);
1158    };
1159    budget.decide()?;
1160    // Each child carries its own copy of the knowledge, linear in the theory:
1161    // what the parent learned holds in both.
1162    let (cut, held) = region.split(atom);
1163    let held = (held, knowledge.clone());
1164    shared.publish_split(index, held, (cut, knowledge), reserve_regions)?;
1165    Ok(Stepped::Split)
1166}

1168/// Decide a leaf as the scalar proposer does.
1169fn leaf<'a>(
1170    shared: &Shared,
1171    original: &IndexedTheory,
1172    region: &Region,
1173    budget: &mut Budget<'a, WorkLease<'a>>,
1174    membership: &mut crate::prepared_reduct::State,
1175    report: &mut WorkerReport,
1176) -> Result<Option<Interpretation>, Incomplete> {
1177    let candidate = super::regions::leaf_interpretation(original.theory(), region)?;
1178    shared
1179        .candidates
1180        .fetch_update(Ordering::Relaxed, Ordering::Relaxed, |count| {
1181            (count < shared.limits.max_candidates).then(|| count + 1)
1182        })
1183        .map_err(|_| Incomplete::CandidateLimit)?;
1184    report.statistics.candidates += 1;
1185    let queries_before = report.statistics.countermodel_queries;
1186    let countermodels_before = report.statistics.countermodels;
1187    let decision: Result<Decision, Incomplete> = if let Some(certificate) = &shared.certificate {
1188        let mut search = budget.statistics;
1189        let wanted = certificate
1190            .checking_work_bound()?
1191            .min(shared.limits.max_verification_work);
1192        let reservation = budget.quota.reserve(wanted)?;
1193        let allowance = reservation.allowance();
1194        let mut limits = shared.limits;
1195        limits.max_verification_work = allowance;
1196        if allowance < wanted {
1197            // Only a shortage of global work lowers the search ceiling.
1198            // A complete reservation preserves a stricter verification
1199            // ceiling's own error attribution.
1200            limits.search.max_work = search
1201                .work
1202                .checked_add(allowance)
1203                .ok_or(Incomplete::CounterOverflow)?;
1204        }
1205        let verdict = certified::classify(
1206            certificate,
1207            &candidate,
1208            limits,
1209            budget.cancellation,
1210            &mut report.statistics,
1211            &mut search,
1212        );
1213        reservation.finish(search.work - budget.statistics.work)?;
1214        budget.statistics = search;
1215        verdict.map(Decision::from)
1216    } else {
1217        membership
1218            .check(
1219                original.theory(),
1220                Some(original),
1221                &candidate,
1222                shared.limits,
1223                budget,
1224                &mut report.statistics,
1225            )
1226            .map(Decision::from)
1227    };
1228    // Query entry is a receipt even when membership checking stops before
1229    // producing a verdict. Publish it before propagating that failure.
1230    Live::add(
1231        &shared.live.countermodel_queries,
1232        report.statistics.countermodel_queries - queries_before,
1233    );
1234    Live::add(
1235        &shared.live.countermodels,
1236        report.statistics.countermodels - countermodels_before,
1237    );
1238    match decision? {
1239        Decision::Stable => Ok(Some(candidate)),
1240        Decision::Refuted => Ok(None),
1241        Decision::Invalid => Err(Incomplete::InvalidWitness),
1242    }
1243}
