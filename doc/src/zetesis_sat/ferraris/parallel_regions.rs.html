1//! Regions walked by several workers at once, sharing immutable preparation,
2//! a cumulative allowance and a work-stealing frontier of regions still to visit.
3//!
4//! The walk begins on the coordinator, the calling thread, which steps slot
5//! 0's deque itself and returns the models it verifies, in the scalar walk's
6//! depth-first order. Once the walk has run, in wall time since it began, for
7//! longer than starting the workers costs (its [`Engagement`] delay), the
8//! coordinator starts them all, and its remaining frontier becomes worker 0's
9//! deque. A walk that ends within its delay starts no thread.
10//!
11//! Each worker owns a work-stealing deque of regions with their knowledge, a
12//! lease on the enumeration's shared allowance that serves its consecutive
13//! regions, and its own evaluation workspace. The enumeration builds the
14//! original-theory index before the first proposal and hands each worker a
15//! clone when it is spawned; candidate and reduct traversals share that
16//! authenticated index but never their mutable knowledge. A worker pops a
17//! region from its own deque, narrows it from the knowledge it carries, drops
18//! it when refuted, splits it otherwise and pushes both children back onto its
19//! deque; a worker whose deque is empty steals a
20//! region from a peer. At a leaf it decides membership as the scalar proposer
21//! does, by the class certificate when one applies and else by the
22//! proper-subset query as a region tree; a stable model is sent to the
23//! enumeration. The regions partition the candidate space exactly
24//! (`Cube.split_partition`, `Cube.split_disjoint` of `Search.lean`), so every
25//! leaf is visited once, by the coordinator or a worker, whatever the
26//! interleaving. Before engagement models arrive in the scalar walk's order;
27//! afterwards their order is the schedule's and is not a property of the result.
28//!
29//! A restriction added while the workers run narrows the regions not yet
30//! visited: each worker reads the restrictions before a narrowing, and a
31//! region reached before a restriction existed gets fresh knowledge under
32//! it. A work, decision or candidate ceiling is shared: the first worker
33//! to exhaust it raises the stop, the others stop at their next charge or
34//! their next region, and the enumeration is incomplete. The models the
35//! workers verified before they stopped are delivered first and the stop
36//! after them, so a leaf admitted under the candidate ceiling is never
37//! lost to a worker the ceiling refused. A cancellation observed by the
38//! coordinator can end its pull immediately; explicit stop or drop joins any
39//! remaining workers. The region counts and reading work, the candidates,
40//! the countermodel counts and the phase timings are the live counters,
41//! current while the walk runs; the coordinator's certificate and reduct
42//! receipts are merged after each of its pulls, and the workers' when they
43//! have finished, so a snapshot taken while workers run lacks theirs. Phase
44//! timings, when enabled, are the coordinator's and the workers' own
45//! narrowing and leaf decisions summed, so they may exceed the wall time of
46//! the walk.
47//!
48//! Each deque has its own mutex: local removal locks only that deque, a split
49//! publication locks that deque and then, after releasing it, the idle gate to
50//! wake one idle worker, and a thief skips a busy peer. Narrowing, payload cloning, reduct
51//! checks and model sends hold no queue lock. Slot growth is fallible and occurs
52//! before either child is published. An atomic counter of unresolved regions
53//! carries termination — a split raises it before pushing the children, a
54//! resolved region lowers it once, and an unsuccessful thief stops only once
55//! that counter has reached zero. An atomic closed flag, set on the first stop
56//! or when the enumeration stops listening, halts the others at their next region.
57//! Idle workers wait at a gate that the last resolution and every close open, and
58//! that a publication opens for one waiter, so none sleeps out its timed wait
59//! after the walk has ended or while a region it could take is published. The
60//! timed wait bounds how late an idle worker sees a cancellation, and how late it
61//! finds a region left in a deque that was busy when it last looked.
62//!
63//! A local depth-first walk keeps at most one older sibling per ancestor and
64//! the two newest children. Each split decides another atom, and a worker steals
65//! only with an empty deque, so each deque holds at most `atom_count + 1` entries.
66//! Storage grows with the observed depth rather than reserving that bound for
67//! every worker. Capacity and payloads are released after joining. Fallible slot
68//! growth does not make region or knowledge cloning fallible.
69//! Each worker may retain one refuted region's knowledge allocation until its
70//! next split or exit. Copying the current parent's complete knowledge into it
71//! reuses storage without retaining any conclusions from the refuted region.
72//! This adds at most one knowledge bundle per worker; copying remains linear
73//! in the payload, and queued children own independent arrays.

105/// thread's start, idle spinning and wakes, and its join, on a walk too short

112/// When a parallel region walk starts its workers.
113///
114/// A walk begins at the first pull, once the enumeration's original index
115/// is built. It then runs on the calling thread: its coordinator steps the
116/// region tree in the scalar walk's depth-first order and returns the models it
117/// verifies. Its workers start once the walk has run for its engagement delay,
118/// measured in wall time from the walk's beginning. The caller's own time
119/// between pulls counts toward the delay, so a caller doing heavy work per model
120/// can carry a walk past it. The delay is checked on each pull and between
121/// the coordinator's steps. A step in progress or time between pulls can
122/// postpone engagement. A walk that ends within its delay starts no thread.
123///
124/// The answer family is the same under every engagement; what differs is the
125/// order in which models arrive, the threads started and the time taken.
126#[non_exhaustive]
127#[derive(Clone, Copy, Debug, Default, PartialEq, Eq)]
128pub enum Engagement {
129    /// The default delay, `w × c` for `w` configured workers, where `c` is the
130    /// measured wall cost of engaging one region worker on the reference host,
131    /// recorded in the repository's observation records. The delay governs
132    /// engagement; it does not bound elapsed-time differences between
133    /// independently scheduled runs.
134    #[default]
135    AfterStartCost,
136    /// No delay: the walk begins and starts every worker at the first pull,
137    /// before any step.
138    Immediate,
139    /// A delay of the given duration. A delay whose deadline, counted from the
140    /// walk's beginning, overflows the monotonic clock never engages:
141    /// `After(Duration::MAX)` keeps the whole walk on the calling thread.
142    After(Duration),
143}
144
145impl Engagement {
146    /// The delay this engagement sets for `workers` configured workers.
147    fn delay(self, workers: usize) -> Duration {
148        match self {
149            Self::AfterStartCost => ENGAGEMENT_COST_PER_WORKER
150                .saturating_mul(u32::try_from(workers).unwrap_or(u32::MAX)),
151            Self::Immediate => Duration::ZERO,
152            Self::After(delay) => delay,
153        }
154    }
155}

192/// What ends the walk — every region resolved, or a close — and the gate idle
193/// workers wait at for either.
194///
195/// The count and the flag are atomics because the region loop reads them for
196/// every region; the gate guards the number of idle waiters. Every transition
197/// that ends the walk changes its atomic first and then takes the gate to wake
198/// the waiters, and an idle worker takes the gate and re-checks both conditions,
199/// and the peers' deques, before it waits. Whichever reaches the gate first, no
200/// idle worker waits past the end of the walk: a worker that takes the gate after
201/// the ending transition sees it, and one that took the gate before is already
202/// waiting when the wake comes. A publication pushes its children, releases its
203/// deque and then takes the gate to wake one waiter, so by the same argument no
204/// waiter misses a region published after its re-check. Zero is terminal, since
205/// a region in hand counts until it is resolved. Every wait stays bounded by its
206/// timeout, so correctness never rests on a wake: the timeout is how an idle
207/// worker sees a cancellation, or a region left in a deque its re-check found
208/// busy.
209struct Termination {
210    /// Created-but-unresolved regions across every worker's deque and hand: a
211    /// split adds one (before pushing its children), a refuted or decided
212    /// region subtracts one. It starts at one for the root and reaches zero
213    /// exactly when the whole frontier is resolved; a read of zero is a true
214    /// zero, so a worker that finds every deque empty and reads zero is done.
215    outstanding: AtomicUsize,
216    /// A stop was raised (error or cancellation) or the enumeration stopped
217    /// listening; workers check it before each region and while idle, and
218    /// exit. Normal termination is `outstanding` reaching zero.
219    closed: AtomicBool,
220    /// The number of idle workers waiting on `idle`. Held to re-check the
221    /// walk's end and the peers' deques before an idle wait, to wake one waiter
222    /// after a publication, and to wake every waiter after the walk ends.
223    gate: Mutex<usize>,
224    /// Idle workers wait here for a published region or the end of the walk.
225    idle: Condvar,
226}
227
228impl Termination {
229    /// The root is the one outstanding region until it is split.
230    fn new() -> Self {
231        Self {
232            outstanding: AtomicUsize::new(1),
233            closed: AtomicBool::new(false),
234            gate: Mutex::new(0),
235            idle: Condvar::new(),
236        }
237    }
238
239    /// A split replaces its parent by two children: count the net gain of one
240    /// before either child becomes visible.
241    fn grow(&self) {
242        self.outstanding.fetch_add(1, Ordering::AcqRel);
243    }
244
245    /// A refuted or decided region leaves the frontier. The last one ends the
246    /// walk and wakes every idle worker.
247    fn resolve(&self) {
248        let previous = self.outstanding.fetch_sub(1, Ordering::AcqRel);
249        debug_assert_ne!(previous, 0, "a region was resolved twice");
250        if previous == 1 {
251            self.wake_idle();
252        }
253    }
254
255    /// Close the walk: workers take no further region, and idle workers wake.
256    fn close(&self) {
257        self.closed.store(true, Ordering::Release);
258        self.wake_idle();
259    }
260
261    fn is_resolved(&self) -> bool {
262        self.outstanding.load(Ordering::Acquire) == 0
263    }
264
265    fn is_closed(&self) -> bool {
266        self.closed.load(Ordering::Acquire)
267    }
268
269    fn has_ended(&self) -> bool {
270        self.is_closed() || self.is_resolved()
271    }
272
273    /// Wake one idle worker, if any waits, after a publication.
274    fn wake_one(&self) {
275        let waiting = self.gate.lock().unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner);
276        if *waiting != 0 {
277            self.idle.notify_one();
278        }
279    }
280
281    /// Wake every idle worker, after the transition that ended the walk.
282    fn wake_idle(&self) {
283        let _gate = self.gate.lock().unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner);
284        self.idle.notify_all();
285    }
286}

304/// One phase's calls and elapsed nanoseconds, summed over the workers.
305#[derive(Default)]
306struct LivePhase {
307    calls: AtomicU64,
308    nanos: AtomicU64,
309    /// Sticky measurement incompleteness, independent of semantic coverage.
310    overflowed: AtomicBool,
311}
312
313impl LivePhase {
314    /// Keep each counter monotone, saturating only after marking the
315    /// measurement incomplete. Live snapshots read independent atomics;
316    /// after workers join, the totals are exact or explicitly overflowed.
317    fn add(&self, counter: &AtomicU64, amount: u64) {
318        if amount == 0 {
319            return;
320        }
321        if counter
322            .fetch_update(Ordering::Relaxed, Ordering::Relaxed, |value| {
323                value.checked_add(amount)
324            })
325            .is_err()
326        {
327            self.overflowed.store(true, Ordering::Relaxed);
328            // Every writer only increases this counter. An overflow makes
329            // MAX absorbing, including concurrent successful additions.
330            counter.store(u64::MAX, Ordering::Relaxed);
331        }
332    }
333}
334
335impl Live {
336    fn add(counter: &AtomicU64, amount: u64) {
337        counter.fetch_add(amount, Ordering::Relaxed);
338    }
339    fn read(counter: &AtomicU64) -> u64 {
340        counter.load(Ordering::Relaxed)
341    }
342
343    /// Add what a worker measured since its last report.
344    fn add_timings(&self, before: &SearchPhaseTimings, after: &SearchPhaseTimings) {
345        for (live, (before, after)) in self.timings.iter().zip(phases(before).zip(phases(after))) {
346            // A worker can become incomplete without changing its retained
347            // numeric prefix, so propagate its flag even for a zero delta.
348            if before.overflowed || after.overflowed {
349                live.overflowed.store(true, Ordering::Relaxed);
350            }
351            let calls = after.calls.saturating_sub(before.calls);
352            let elapsed = after.elapsed.saturating_sub(before.elapsed);
353            let nanos = u64::try_from(elapsed.as_nanos()).unwrap_or_else(|_| {
354                live.overflowed.store(true, Ordering::Relaxed);
355                u64::MAX
356            });
357            live.add(&live.calls, calls);
358            live.add(&live.nanos, nanos);
359        }
360    }
361
362    fn timings(&self) -> SearchPhaseTimings {
363        let read = |live: &LivePhase| PhaseMeasurement {
364            calls: Self::read(&live.calls),
365            elapsed: Duration::from_nanos(Self::read(&live.nanos)),
366            overflowed: live.overflowed.load(Ordering::Relaxed),
367        };
368        let [
369            candidates,
370            original_validation,
371            reduct,
372            reduct_preparation,
373            certified,
374        ] = &self.timings;
375        SearchPhaseTimings {
376            candidates: read(candidates),
377            original_validation: read(original_validation),
378            reduct: read(reduct),
379            reduct_preparation: read(reduct_preparation),
380            certified: read(certified),
381        }
382    }
383}

397impl Shared {
398    fn queue(&self, index: usize) -> MutexGuard<'_, VecDeque<Entry>> {
399        self.queues[index]
400            .lock()
401            .unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner)
402    }
403
404    fn take_local(&self, index: usize) -> Option<Entry> {
405        self.queue(index).pop_back()
406    }
407
408    /// Take the oldest region of a peer's deque, trying peers in round-robin
409    /// order and skipping a busy one, so one owner's growth cannot block
410    /// stealing from the others. No two queue locks overlap, and none is
411    /// waited for.
412    fn steal(&self, index: usize) -> Option<Entry> {
413        for offset in 1..self.workers {
414            let mut queue = match self.queues[(index + offset) % self.workers].try_lock() {
415                Ok(queue) => queue,
416                Err(TryLockError::Poisoned(error)) => error.into_inner(),
417                Err(TryLockError::WouldBlock) => continue,
418            };
419            if let Some(entry) = queue.pop_front() {
420                return Some(entry);
421            }
422        }
423        None
424    }
425
426    /// Wait at the gate for a published region or the end of the walk, for at
427    /// most `timeout`. Under the gate, the walk's end and the peers' deques are
428    /// re-checked first; a region found then is returned at once. `None` does
429    /// not mean the walk ended: the caller re-checks.
430    fn wait_for_work(&self, index: usize, timeout: Duration) -> Option<Entry> {
431        let mut waiting = self
432            .termination
433            .gate
434            .lock()
435            .unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner);
436        if self.termination.has_ended() {
437            return None;
438        }
439        if let Some(entry) = self.steal(index) {
440            return Some(entry);
441        }
442        *waiting += 1;
443        let (mut waiting, _) = self
444            .termination
445            .idle
446            .wait_timeout(waiting, timeout)
447            .unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner);
448        *waiting -= 1;
449        None
450    }
451
452    /// Prepare space for both children before the count or either queue entry
453    /// changes. The injected reservation is the sole fallible storage effect;
454    /// success must reserve `additional` slots, as `reserve_regions` does.
455    fn publish_split(
456        &self,
457        index: usize,
458        held: Entry,
459        cut: Entry,
460        reserve: impl FnOnce(&mut VecDeque<Entry>, usize) -> Result<(), Incomplete>,
461    ) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
462        let mut queue = self.queue(index);
463        reserve(&mut queue, 2)?;
464        // The parent is still outstanding. Count its net gain before making
465        // either child visible; the reserved pushes cannot allocate.
466        self.termination.grow();
467        queue.push_back(held);
468        queue.push_back(cut);
469        // Release the deque before taking the gate: a waiter holds the gate
470        // while it tries the deques, and never waits for a queue lock.
471        drop(queue);
472        self.termination.wake_one();
473        Ok(())
474    }
475
476    /// Close for a normal end of work (no error): workers exit when they next
477    /// check, idle ones at once, without recording a stop.
478    fn close(&self) {
479        self.termination.close();
480    }
481
482    /// Raise a stop: work closes, and the first stop is the one reported.
483    fn stop(&self, error: Incomplete) {
484        self.stopped
485            .lock()
486            .unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner)
487            .get_or_insert(error);
488        self.termination.close();
489    }
490
491    /// The first stop raised, if any.
492    fn stopped(&self) -> Option<Incomplete> {
493        *self.stopped.lock().unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner)
494    }
495}

670    /// knowledge; its first narrowing creates each knowledge slot. The walk
671    /// begins on the first call to `propose`, once the enumeration's
672    /// certificates are configured and its original index is built; the
673    /// workers start when it has run for its engagement delay.
674    pub(crate) fn new(
675        theory: &Theory,
676        workers: NonZeroUsize,
677        engagement: Engagement,
678        limits: Limits,
679        cancellation: Cancellation,
680        budget: &mut Budget<'_>,
681    ) -> Result<Self, Incomplete> {
682        let delay = engagement.delay(workers.get());
683        let super::regions::Opened { parts, statistics } = super::regions::open(theory, budget)?;
684        let (sender, receiver) = sync_channel(workers.get() * CHANNEL_SLACK);
685        let root = (
686            Region::all_open(theory.atom_count()),
687            CandidateKnowledge::default(),
688        );
689        let mut queues = crate::search::storage(workers.get())?;
690        let mut first = VecDeque::new();
691        reserve_regions(&mut first, 1)?;
692        first.push_back(root);
693        queues.push(Mutex::new(first));
694        for _ in 1..workers.get() {
695            queues.push(Mutex::new(VecDeque::new()));
696        }
697        Ok(Self {
698            shared: Arc::new(Shared {
699                parts,
700                certificate: None,
701                filter: None,
702                restrictions: RwLock::new(Conditions::default()),
703                queues,
704                termination: Termination::new(),
705                stopped: Mutex::new(None),
706                budget: SharedBudget::new(limits.search, budget.statistics),

708                cancellation,

750    /// Close the walk, release the coordinator's state and blocked senders,
751    /// then join before exposing the final source-accounting receipt. The
752    /// caller's token is unchanged.
753    pub(crate) fn stop(&mut self) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {

757    /// The one teardown, the same in every state, run by `stop`, by drop and
758    /// by every failure: it tears down whatever the walk owns and keeps the
759    /// record. Each step is a no-op when nothing is owned, so a second
760    /// teardown changes nothing and reports the same stop.
761    fn teardown(&mut self) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
762        self.shared.close();
763        if let Err(error) = self.release_coordinator() {
764            self.shared.stop(error);
765        }
766        drop(self.receiver.take());
767        drop(self.sender.take());
768        let joined = self.join();
769        self.shared.stopped().map_or(joined, Err)
770    }

772    /// Release the coordinator's retained state through a contained drop: a
773    /// checker whose teardown panics becomes `WorkerPanicked`, never
774    /// exhaustion, as in the scalar walk.
775    fn release_coordinator(&mut self) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
776        self.coordinator.take().map_or(Ok(()), |cell| {
777            std::panic::catch_unwind(std::panic::AssertUnwindSafe(move || drop(cell)))
778                .map_err(|_| Incomplete::WorkerPanicked)
779        })

845    /// Restrict every region not yet visited to the classical models of
846    /// `restriction`; the workers read it before their next narrowing.
847    /// Indexing it is charged to the shared allowance the workers draw on,
848    /// and the enumeration's counters take the charge at once.
849    pub(crate) fn restrict(
850        &mut self,
851        restriction: &Theory,
852        budget: &mut Budget<'_>,
853    ) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
854        if matches!(self.record, WalkRecord::NotBegun) {
855            self.synchronize_budget(budget.statistics)?;
856        }
857        let narrower = super::original_index::prepare(
858            restriction,
859            self.shared.limits.max_native_narrowing_bytes,
860            budget.cancellation,
861        )?;
862        self.shared.budget.charge(narrower.work())?;
863        self.account(budget);
864        self.statistics.counts.work += narrower.work();
865        let mut restrictions = self
866            .shared
867            .restrictions
868            .write()
869            .unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner);
870        restrictions
871            .permanent
872            .try_reserve(1)
873            .map_err(|_| Incomplete::Allocation)?;
874        restrictions
875            .permanent
876            .push(Arc::new((restriction.clone(), narrower)));
877        Ok(())
878    }

880    /// A worker's existing snapshot may finish under the old generation;
881    /// future snapshots see this replacement and all permanent restrictions.
882    ///
883    /// This follows `Bound::tightened`'s order but stays separate from it: the
884    /// charge lands on the workers' shared allowance, synchronized first, and
885    /// is accounted to the caller's budget even when refused.
886    pub(crate) fn tighten(
887        &mut self,
888        restriction: &Theory,
889        generation: u64,
890        budget: &mut Budget<'_>,
891    ) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
892        budget.cancellation.poll()?;
893        if matches!(self.record, WalkRecord::NotBegun) {
894            self.synchronize_budget(budget.statistics)?;
895        }
896        let bound = Bound::prepare(
897            restriction,
898            generation,
899            self.shared.limits.max_native_narrowing_bytes,
900            budget.cancellation,
901        )?;
902        let charged = self.shared.budget.charge(bound.work());
903        self.account(budget);
904        charged?;
905        let work = self
906            .statistics
907            .counts
908            .work
909            .checked_add(bound.work())
910            .ok_or(Incomplete::CounterOverflow)?;
911        self.statistics.counts.work = work;
912        budget.cancellation.poll()?;
913        let previous = self
914            .shared
915            .restrictions
916            .write()
917            .unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner)
918            .bound
919            .replace(bound);
920        // Release retired storage outside the readers' snapshot lock.
921        drop(previous);
922        Ok(())
923    }

950    /// The next verified stable model, or `None` once the root is covered.
951    /// Begins the walk on the first call and steps it on the coordinator until
952    /// the engagement delay has passed, then starts the workers, handing each
953    /// a clone of `index`, the enumeration's one original index, with the
954    /// certificate the enumeration held when the walk began, timing their
955    /// phases when `timed`. A raised stop is returned once every model already
956    /// verified has been returned, and on every later call, `stop()` included;
957    /// a walk closed without a stop returns `ClosedEnumerator`.
958    pub(crate) fn propose(
959        &mut self,
960        index: &Arc<IndexedTheory>,
961        certificate: Option<&Arc<Certification>>,
962        timed: bool,
963        budget: &mut Budget<'_>,
964    ) -> Result<Option<Interpretation>, Incomplete> {
965        self.propose_with(index, certificate, timed, budget, &mut |deadline| {
966            Instant::now() >= deadline
967        })
968    }

970    /// `propose`, asking `deadline_passed` whether the engagement deadline has
971    /// passed: on entry to each coordinator iteration and after each step that
972    /// returned no model. A walk without a deadline never asks it.
973    fn propose_with(
974        &mut self,
975        index: &Arc<IndexedTheory>,
976        certificate: Option<&Arc<Certification>>,
977        timed: bool,
978        budget: &mut Budget<'_>,
979        deadline_passed: &mut impl FnMut(Instant) -> bool,
980    ) -> Result<Option<Interpretation>, Incomplete> {
981        if self.exhausted {
982            return Ok(None);
983        }
984        if matches!(self.record, WalkRecord::NotBegun) {
985            self.begin(certificate, timed, budget)?;
986        }
987        if matches!(self.record, WalkRecord::Unengaged { .. }) {
988            match self.pull_coordinator(index, budget, deadline_passed)? {
989                Coordinated::Answer(answer) => return Ok(answer),
990                Coordinated::Engage => self.engage(index, budget)?,
991            }
992        }
993        loop {
994            budget.cancellation.poll()?;
995            // A closed walk answers alike in every state: its recorded stop
996            // first, and else the closed enumerator.
997            let Some(receiver) = self.receiver.as_ref() else {
998                return Err(self
999                    .shared
1000                    .stopped()
1001                    .unwrap_or(Incomplete::ClosedEnumerator));
1002            };
1003            match receiver.recv_timeout(IDLE_WAIT) {
1004                Ok(model) => {
1005                    self.account(budget);
1006                    return Ok(Some(model));
1007                }
1008                Err(std::sync::mpsc::RecvTimeoutError::Timeout) => {}
1009                Err(std::sync::mpsc::RecvTimeoutError::Disconnected) => {
1010                    // Every worker has finished: the channel holds nothing
1011                    // more, so the stop, if one was raised, follows every
1012                    // model the workers verified.
1013                    let joined = self.join();
1014                    self.account(budget);
1015                    if let Some(error) = self.shared.stopped() {
1016                        return Err(error);
1017                    }
1018                    joined?;
1019                    // Clean completion with no stop: every region created was
1020                    // resolved, so the frontier counter is exactly zero. This
1021                    // guards the accounting-regression class (a missing or
1022                    // doubled decrement, or a total premature exit that abandons
1023                    // regions) that the exact-family check cannot see.
1024                    debug_assert_eq!(
1025                        self.shared.termination.outstanding.load(Ordering::Acquire),
1026                        0,
1027                        "clean completion left regions outstanding"
1028                    );
1029                    self.exhausted = true;
1030                    return Ok(None);
1031                }
1032            }
1033        }
1034    }

1045    /// Begin the walk at the first pull: poll cancellation and refuse a closed
1046    /// walk, leaving it unbegun in either case; synchronize the budget and install the
1047    /// certificate and the timing flag, while no other party holds the shared
1048    /// structure; record the beginning and the deadline; and create the
1049    /// coordinator's retained state unless the delay is zero. A timing enable
1050    /// made after the walk has begun is ignored for the whole walk.
1051    fn begin(
1052        &mut self,
1053        certificate: Option<&Arc<Certification>>,
1054        timed: bool,
1055        budget: &mut Budget<'_>,
1056    ) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
1057        budget.cancellation.poll()?;
1058        if self.shared.termination.is_closed() {
1059            return Err(self
1060                .shared
1061                .stopped()
1062                .unwrap_or(Incomplete::ClosedEnumerator));
1063        }
1064        let shared = Arc::get_mut(&mut self.shared).ok_or(Incomplete::InvalidWitness)?;
1065        shared.budget = SharedBudget::new(shared.limits.search, budget.statistics);
1066        // The certificate is shared read-only; the Arc is cloned into the
1067        // shared structure before any other party holds it.
1068        shared.certificate = certificate.map(Arc::clone);
1069        shared.timed = timed;
1070        let since = Instant::now();
1071        let deadline = self.delay.and_then(|delay| since.checked_add(delay));
1072        if deadline.is_none() {
1073            self.delay = None;
1074        }
1075        self.record = WalkRecord::Unengaged {
1076            since,
1077            deadline,
1078            elapsed: Duration::ZERO,
1079            walking: Duration::ZERO,
1080        };
1081        if self.delay != Some(Duration::ZERO) {
1082            self.coordinator = Some(CoordinatorCell::new(Arc::clone(&self.shared), |shared| {
1083                Coordinator {
1084                    workspaces: Workspaces::new(),
1085                    report: WorkerReport::for_walk(shared),
1086                    reported: SearchPhaseTimings::default(),
1087                }
1088            }));
1089        }
1090        Ok(())
1091    }

1093    /// One coordinator pull: its walk, then the end of its coordinator part
1094    /// (the lease settled and dropped, the membership receipts merged and
1095    /// taken, the record updated if it stepped, the shared allowance
1096    /// accounted), then the exit's own effect.
1097    fn pull_coordinator(
1098        &mut self,
1099        index: &Arc<IndexedTheory>,
1100        budget: &mut Budget<'_>,
1101        deadline_passed: &mut impl FnMut(Instant) -> bool,
1102    ) -> Result<Coordinated, Incomplete> {
1103        let WalkRecord::Unengaged {
1104            since, deadline, ..
1105        } = self.record
1106        else {
1107            return Err(Incomplete::InvalidWitness);
1108        };
1109        let entry = Instant::now();
1110        let caller = budget.cancellation;
1111        let merged = &mut self.merged;
1112        let original: &IndexedTheory = index;
1113        let (exit, stepped, merge) = match self.coordinator.as_mut() {
1114            Some(cell) => cell.with_dependent_mut(|shared, coordinator| {
1115                coordinate(
1116                    shared,
1117                    Some(coordinator),
1118                    original,
1119                    caller,
1120                    deadline,
1121                    deadline_passed,
1122                    merged,
1123                )
1124            }),
1125            None => coordinate(
1126                &self.shared,
1127                None,
1128                original,
1129                caller,
1130                deadline,
1131                deadline_passed,
1132                merged,
1133            ),
1134        };
1135        if stepped {
1136            let now = Instant::now();
1137            if let WalkRecord::Unengaged {
1138                elapsed, walking, ..
1139            } = &mut self.record
1140            {
1141                *walking = walking.saturating_add(now.saturating_duration_since(entry));
1142                *elapsed = now.saturating_duration_since(since);
1143            }
1144        }
1145        self.account(budget);
1146        if let Err(error) = merge {
1147            // Raised as join raises its merge failure. A model in hand is
1148            // still returned; the stop follows on the next pull.
1149            self.shared.stop(error);
1150            let _ = self.teardown();
1151            return match exit {
1152                Exit::Model(model) => Ok(Coordinated::Answer(Some(model))),
1153                _ => Err(self.shared.stopped().unwrap_or(error)),
1154            };
1155        }
1156        match exit {
1157            Exit::Model(model) => Ok(Coordinated::Answer(Some(model))),
1158            Exit::Resolved => {
1159                if let Err(error) = self.release_coordinator() {
1160                    self.shared.stop(error);
1161                    let _ = self.teardown();
1162                    return Err(self.shared.stopped().unwrap_or(error));
1163                }
1164                self.release_frontier();
1165                debug_assert_eq!(
1166                    self.shared.termination.outstanding.load(Ordering::Acquire),
1167                    0,
1168                    "a resolved walk left regions outstanding"
1169                );
1170                self.exhausted = true;
1171                Ok(Coordinated::Answer(None))
1172            }
1173            Exit::Deadline => Ok(Coordinated::Engage),
1174            Exit::Cancelled(error) => Err(error),
1175            Exit::Closed => Err(self
1176                .shared
1177                .stopped()
1178                .unwrap_or(Incomplete::ClosedEnumerator)),
1179            Exit::Failed | Exit::Panicked => {
1180                let _ = self.teardown();
1181                Err(self.shared.stopped().unwrap_or(Incomplete::InvalidWitness))
1182            }
1183        }
1184    }

1201    /// Start the workers: release the coordinator's state, then reserve the
1202    /// handles and spawn every slot in order, so worker 0 continues the
1203    /// coordinator's depth-first walk from deque 0 and the others steal from
1204    /// it. Thread creation is an execution boundary: a refused reservation or
1205    /// spawn records the threads actually launched, raises `Allocation`,
1206    /// and runs the teardown, which joins the launched subset and discards the
1207    /// models it queued, before the incomplete result escapes.
1208    fn engage_with(
1209        &mut self,
1210        budget: &mut Budget<'_>,
1211        mut spawn: impl FnMut(
1212            Arc<Shared>,
1213            usize,
1214            SyncSender<Interpretation>,
1215        ) -> std::io::Result<JoinHandle<Option<WorkerReport>>>,
1216    ) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
1217        let WalkRecord::Unengaged { since, walking, .. } = self.record else {
1218            return Err(Incomplete::InvalidWitness);
1219        };
1220        if let Err(error) = self.release_coordinator() {
1221            self.shared.stop(error);
1222            let _ = self.teardown();
1223            self.account(budget);
1224            return Err(self.shared.stopped().unwrap_or(error));
1225        }
1226        let start = Instant::now();
1227        let launched = self.launch(&mut spawn);
1228        self.record = WalkRecord::Engaged {
1229            after: start.saturating_duration_since(since),
1230            walking,
1231            launched: self.handles.len(),
1232            spawn: start.elapsed(),
1233        };
1234        if let Err(error) = launched {
1235            self.shared.stop(error);
1236            let _ = self.teardown();
1237            self.account(budget);
1238            return Err(error);
1239        }
1240        Ok(())
1241    }

1243    /// Reserve the handles, then spawn each slot and push its handle as it is
1244    /// created, so the handles are the threads actually launched at every
1245    /// point. The original sender is dropped on return.
1246    fn launch(
1247        &mut self,
1248        spawn: &mut impl FnMut(
1249            Arc<Shared>,
1250            usize,
1251            SyncSender<Interpretation>,
1252        ) -> std::io::Result<JoinHandle<Option<WorkerReport>>>,
1253    ) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
1254        self.handles
1255            .try_reserve(self.shared.workers)
1256            .map_err(|_| Incomplete::Allocation)?;
1257        let sender = self.sender.take().ok_or(Incomplete::ClosedEnumerator)?;
1258        for index in 0..self.shared.workers {
1259            let handle = spawn(Arc::clone(&self.shared), index, sender.clone())
1260                .map_err(|_| Incomplete::Allocation)?;
1261            self.handles.push(handle);
1262        }
1263        Ok(())
1264    }
1265
1266    /// Wait for every worker and merge its membership receipts; its region
1267    /// receipts are already in the live counters.
1268    ///
1269    /// # Errors
1270    /// A merged count beyond its width is the counter refusal.
1271    fn join(&mut self) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
1272        let mut failure = None;
1273        for handle in self.handles.drain(..) {
1274            let result = match handle.join() {
1275                Ok(Some(report)) => merge_membership(&mut self.merged, &report.statistics),
1276                Ok(None) => Ok(()),
1277                Err(_) => Err(Incomplete::WorkerPanicked),
1278            };
1279            if let Err(error) = result {
1280                self.shared.stop(error);
1281                failure.get_or_insert(error);
1282            }
1283        }
1284        // No worker can still own or publish work. Release abandoned payloads
1285        // and high-water slot capacity even if the caller keeps the enumerator.
1286        self.release_frontier();
1287        failure.map_or(Ok(()), Err)
1288    }
1289
1290    /// Release every deque's payloads and capacity, once no party can still
1291    /// own or publish work.
1292    fn release_frontier(&self) {
1293        for index in 0..self.shared.workers {
1294            let pending = std::mem::take(&mut *self.shared.queue(index));
1295            drop(pending);
1296        }
1297    }
1298
1299    /// The shared allowance's spent work and decisions become the
1300    /// enumeration's search counters.
1301    fn account(&self, budget: &mut Budget<'_>) {
1302        let mut spent = SearchStatistics::default();
1303        self.shared.budget.snapshot(&mut spent);
1304        budget.statistics.work = budget.statistics.work.max(spent.work);
1305        budget.statistics.decisions = budget.statistics.decisions.max(spent.decisions);
1306    }
1307}

1333/// Merge a joined worker's certificate and reduct receipts; its candidate,
1334/// query and countermodel counts reached the live counters as it went, and
1335/// the coordinator counts the stable models it returns. A peak is the largest
1336/// of any one workspace's. A sum beyond its counter's width is the counter
1337/// refusal.
1338fn merge_membership(into: &mut Statistics, from: &Statistics) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
1339    fn add(counter: &mut u64, value: u64) -> Result<(), Incomplete> {
1340        *counter = counter
1341            .checked_add(value)
1342            .ok_or(Incomplete::CounterOverflow)?;
1343        Ok(())
1344    }
1345    add(&mut into.reduct.original_work, from.reduct.original_work)?;
1346    into.reduct.peak_workspace_bytes = into
1347        .reduct
1348        .peak_workspace_bytes
1349        .max(from.reduct.peak_workspace_bytes);
1350    if let Some(from) = from.certified.as_ref() {
1351        let into = into.certified.get_or_insert_default();
1352        add(&mut into.checks, from.checks)?;
1353        add(&mut into.stable, from.stable)?;
1354        add(&mut into.refuted, from.refuted)?;
1355        add(&mut into.failed, from.failed)?;
1356        add(&mut into.checking_work, from.checking_work)?;
1357        into.tight_check_peak_bytes =
1358            match (into.tight_check_peak_bytes, from.tight_check_peak_bytes) {
1359                (Some(a), Some(b)) => Some(a.max(b)),
1360                (a, b) => a.or(b),
1361            };
1362        into.positive_check_peak_bytes = match (
1363            into.positive_check_peak_bytes,
1364            from.positive_check_peak_bytes,
1365        ) {
1366            (Some(a), Some(b)) => Some(a.max(b)),
1367            (a, b) => a.or(b),
1368        };
1369        into.stratified_check_peak_bytes = match (
1370            into.stratified_check_peak_bytes,
1371            from.stratified_check_peak_bytes,
1372        ) {
1373            (Some(a), Some(b)) => Some(a.max(b)),
1374            (a, b) => a.or(b),
1375        };
1376    }
1377    into.reduct.regions.add(from.reduct.regions)
1378}

1380/// One coordinator pull's walk, with its guards in this order at every
1381/// iteration: the caller's cancellation; a closed walk; a resolved frontier;
1382/// the engagement deadline, asked through `deadline_passed`; then a step of
1383/// the newest region of deque 0 through `advance`, under the pull's one lease,
1384/// taken at its first step. A step's error or panic is raised as the shared
1385/// stop before the pull's coordinator part ends, so it is the stop reported.
1386///
1387/// Before returning, the lease is settled and dropped, and unless a step
1388/// panicked the coordinator's certificate and reduct receipts are merged into
1389/// `merged` and taken. Returns the exit, whether the pull stepped, and the
1390/// merge's outcome.
1391///
1392/// Between iterations the outstanding count equals the length of deque 0,
1393/// every other deque is empty, no region is in hand and no worker exists, so
1394/// deque 0 cannot be empty at the step; an empty deque or a missing
1395/// coordinator state is an inconsistency, refused as an invalid witness and
1396/// never taken for exhaustion. Each iteration returns, engages or steps, and
1397/// a step splits or resolves a region of a finite tree, so the pull ends.
1398fn coordinate<'a>(
1399    shared: &'a Shared,
1400    mut coordinator: Option<&mut Coordinator<'a>>,
1401    original: &IndexedTheory,
1402    caller: &Cancellation,
1403    deadline: Option<Instant>,
1404    deadline_passed: &mut impl FnMut(Instant) -> bool,
1405    merged: &mut Statistics,
1406) -> (Exit, bool, Result<(), Incomplete>) {
1407    let mut lease: Option<WorkLease<'a>> = None;
1408    let mut search = SearchStatistics::default();
1409    let mut stepped = false;
1410    let exit = loop {
1411        if let Err(stop) = caller.poll() {
1412            break Exit::Cancelled(stop.into());
1413        }
1414        if shared.termination.is_closed() {
1415            break Exit::Closed;
1416        }
1417        if shared.termination.is_resolved() {
1418            break Exit::Resolved;
1419        }
1420        // With no delay the walk holds no coordinator state and engages before
1421        // any step.
1422        if let Some(deadline) = deadline
1423            && (coordinator.is_none() || deadline_passed(deadline))
1424        {
1425            break Exit::Deadline;
1426        }
1427        let (Some(coordinator), Some(entry)) = (coordinator.as_deref_mut(), shared.take_local(0))
1428        else {
1429            shared.stop(Incomplete::InvalidWitness);
1430            break Exit::Failed;
1431        };
1432        stepped = true;
1433        let quota = lease
1434            .take()
1435            .unwrap_or_else(|| shared.budget.lease(&shared.cancellation));
1436        let mut budget = Budget {
1437            quota,
1438            limits: shared.limits.search,
1439            cancellation: &shared.cancellation,
1440            statistics: search,
1441        };
1442        let outcome = std::panic::catch_unwind(std::panic::AssertUnwindSafe(|| {
1443            advance(
1444                shared,
1445                original,
1446                entry,
1447                0,
1448                &mut coordinator.workspaces,
1449                &mut budget,
1450                (&mut coordinator.report, &mut coordinator.reported),
1451            )
1452        }));
1453        search = budget.statistics;
1454        lease = Some(budget.quota);
1455        match outcome {
1456            Ok(Ok(Stepped::Split | Stepped::Resolved(None))) => {}
1457            Ok(Ok(Stepped::Resolved(Some(model)))) => break Exit::Model(model),
1458            Ok(Err(error)) => {
1459                shared.stop(error);
1460                break Exit::Failed;
1461            }
1462            Err(_) => {
1463                shared.stop(Incomplete::WorkerPanicked);
1464                break Exit::Panicked;
1465            }
1466        }
1467    };
1468    if let Some(lease) = lease {
1469        lease.settle();
1470    }
1471    let merge = match (coordinator, &exit) {
1472        // A panicking pull's receipts are discarded with the coordinator's
1473        // state, as a panicked worker's report is.
1474        (_, Exit::Panicked) => Ok(()),
1475        (Some(coordinator), _) if stepped => coordinator.report.merge_into(merged),
1476        _ => Ok(()),
1477    };
1478    (exit, stepped, merge)
1479}

1481/// Step one region through `step`, then publish its phase timings to the live
1482/// counters as the difference from the reported baseline, and resolve the
1483/// region once when it is resolved. The workers and the coordinator both
1484/// drive this one unit, so they step alike.
1485fn advance<'a>(
1486    shared: &'a Shared,
1487    original: &IndexedTheory,
1488    entry: Entry,
1489    index: usize,
1490    workspaces: &mut Workspaces<'a>,
1491    budget: &mut Budget<'a, WorkLease<'a>>,
1492    (report, reported): (&mut WorkerReport, &mut SearchPhaseTimings),
1493) -> Result<Stepped, Incomplete> {
1494    let stepped = step(shared, original, entry, index, workspaces, budget, report);
1495    if let Some(measured) = report.statistics.phase_timings {
1496        shared.live.add_timings(reported, &measured);
1497        *reported = measured;
1498    }
1499    // A resolved region leaves the frontier: resolve once, here, so the count
1500    // has exactly one structural decrement point.
1501    if let Ok(Stepped::Resolved(_)) = stepped {
1502        shared.termination.resolve();
1503    }
1504    stepped
1505}

1507/// One worker's walk over `original`, the enumeration's index, until the run
1508/// closes, a stop is raised, or the enumeration stops listening.
1509fn worker(
1510    shared: &Shared,
1511    original: &IndexedTheory,
1512    index: usize,
1513    sender: &SyncSender<Interpretation>,
1514) -> WorkerReport {
1515    let mut report = WorkerReport::for_walk(shared);
1516    let mut reported = SearchPhaseTimings::default();
1517    let mut search = SearchStatistics::default();
1518    let mut workspaces = Workspaces::new();
1519    // One lease serves this worker's consecutive regions, so the shared ledger
1520    // is locked about twice per grant rather than twice per region. It settles
1521    // before this worker steals or waits for another region and before it sends
1522    // a model; a reservation settles it first, and exit or unwinding drops it.
1523    // No unused permit is held where this worker could block.
1524    let mut quota = shared.budget.lease(&shared.cancellation);
1525    loop {
1526        // Honour a stop before taking any region, so a worker with a deep deque
1527        // does not run its whole subtree on after a stop was raised.
1528        if shared.termination.is_closed() {
1529            break;
1530        }
1531        let local = shared.take_local(index);
1532        if local.is_none() {
1533            quota.settle();
1534        }
1535        let Some(entry) = local.or_else(|| find_work(shared, index, IDLE_WAIT)) else {
1536            break;
1537        };
1538        let stepped = {
1539            let mut budget = Budget {
1540                quota,
1541                limits: shared.limits.search,
1542                cancellation: &shared.cancellation,
1543                statistics: search,
1544            };
1545            let result = advance(
1546                shared,
1547                original,
1548                entry,
1549                index,
1550                &mut workspaces,
1551                &mut budget,
1552                (&mut report, &mut reported),
1553            );
1554            search = budget.statistics;
1555            quota = budget.quota;
1556            result
1557        };
1558        match stepped {
1559            // A split's children are on this worker's deque; `outstanding` was
1560            // already incremented before they were pushed.
1561            Ok(Stepped::Split) => {}
1562            // `advance` resolved the region once.
1563            Ok(Stepped::Resolved(model)) => {
1564                if let Some(model) = model {
1565                    quota.settle();
1566                    if sender.send(model).is_err() {
1567                        shared.close();
1568                        break;
1569                    }
1570                }
1571            }
1572            Err(error) => {
1573                shared.stop(error);
1574                break;
1575            }
1576        }
1577    }
1578    report
1579}

1581/// Steal a region from another worker, waiting while any remains. `None` when
1582/// the whole frontier is resolved or a stop closed the run. `idle_wait` bounds
1583/// how long an idle worker waits before it looks for a region again and polls
1584/// cancellation; a publication or the end of the walk ends the wait at once.
1585fn find_work(shared: &Shared, index: usize, idle_wait: Duration) -> Option<Entry> {
1586    let mut idle_rounds = 0u32;
1587    loop {
1588        if shared.termination.is_closed() {
1589            return None;
1590        }
1591        if let Some(entry) = shared.steal(index) {
1592            return Some(entry);
1593        }
1594        // No steal succeeded. Even with busy queues, zero means every created
1595        // region was resolved; a split counts its children before publishing.
1596        if shared.termination.is_resolved() {
1597            return None;
1598        }
1599        // Busy queues and empty queues share the backoff, so lock contention
1600        // cannot postpone cooperative control checks indefinitely.
1601        idle_rounds += 1;
1602        if idle_rounds < SPIN_ROUNDS_BEFORE_WAIT {
1603            std::thread::yield_now();
1604        } else {
1605            if let Some(entry) = shared.wait_for_work(index, idle_wait) {
1606                return Some(entry);
1607            }
1608            if let Err(error) = shared.cancellation.poll() {
1609                shared.stop(error.into());
1610                return None;
1611            }
1612            idle_rounds = 0;
1613        }
1614    }
1615}

1660/// Narrow one region and act on it: refuted, split, or a leaf decided by
1661/// the reduct. A split publishes both children onto this worker's deque.
1662fn step<'a>(
1663    shared: &'a Shared,
1664    original: &IndexedTheory,
1665    (mut region, mut knowledge): Entry,
1666    index: usize,
1667    workspaces: &mut Workspaces<'a>,
1668    budget: &mut Budget<'a, WorkLease<'a>>,
1669    report: &mut WorkerReport,
1670) -> Result<Stepped, Incomplete> {
1671    // The restrictions current when the region is taken, held for its
1672    // narrowing without the lock.
1673    let restrictions = shared
1674        .restrictions
1675        .read()
1676        .unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner)
1677        .clone();
1678    report.regions.regions += 1;
1679    Live::add(&shared.live.regions, 1);
1680    let before = report.regions;
1681    let started = timing::start(report.statistics.phase_timings.as_ref());
1682    let mut check = shared
1683        .filter
1684        .as_ref()
1685        .map(|filter| crate::region_filter::Check {
1686            filter,
1687            worker: &mut workspaces.filter,
1688            timings: &mut report.statistics.phase_timings,
1689        });
1690    let narrowing = super::regions::narrow(
1691        (shared.parts.subject(original.theory()), original.narrower()),
1692        &restrictions,
1693        (&mut region, &mut knowledge),
1694        &mut workspaces.scratch,
1695        budget,
1696        &mut report.regions,
1697        check.as_mut(),
1698    );
1699    timing::finish(
1700        &mut report.statistics.phase_timings,
1701        Phase::Candidates,
1702        started,
1703    );
1704    let after = report.regions;
1705    Live::add(
1706        &shared.live.propagations,
1707        after.propagations - before.propagations,
1708    );
1709    Live::add(&shared.live.held, after.held - before.held);
1710    Live::add(&shared.live.cut, after.cut - before.cut);
1711    Live::add(&shared.live.work, after.work - before.work);
1712    let narrowing = narrowing?;
1713    if narrowing == Narrowing::Refuted {
1714        report.regions.refuted += 1;
1715        Live::add(&shared.live.refuted, 1);
1716        workspaces.retired_knowledge = Some(knowledge);
1717        return Ok(Stepped::Resolved(None));
1718    }
1719    let Some(atom) = region.split_atom() else {
1720        report.regions.leaves += 1;
1721        Live::add(&shared.live.leaves, 1);
1722        return leaf(
1723            shared,
1724            original,
1725            &region,
1726            budget,
1727            &mut workspaces.membership,
1728            &mut workspaces.certificate,
1729            report,
1730        )
1731        .map(Stepped::Resolved);
1732    };
1733    budget.decide()?;
1734    // Each child carries its own copy of the knowledge, linear in the theory:
1735    // what the parent learned holds in both.
1736    let (cut, held) = region.split(atom);
1737    let held = (held, workspaces.copy_knowledge(&knowledge));
1738    shared.publish_split(index, held, (cut, knowledge), reserve_regions)?;
1739    Ok(Stepped::Split)
1740}

1742/// Decide a leaf as the scalar proposer does.
1743fn leaf<'a>(
1744    shared: &Shared,
1745    original: &IndexedTheory,
1746    region: &Region,
1747    budget: &mut Budget<'a, WorkLease<'a>>,
1748    membership: &mut crate::prepared_reduct::State,
1749    workspace: &mut certified::Workspace,
1750    report: &mut WorkerReport,
1751) -> Result<Option<Interpretation>, Incomplete> {
1752    let candidate = super::regions::leaf_interpretation(original.theory(), region)?;
1753    shared
1754        .candidates
1755        .fetch_update(Ordering::Relaxed, Ordering::Relaxed, |count| {
1756            (count < shared.limits.max_candidates).then(|| count + 1)
1757        })
1758        .map_err(|_| Incomplete::CandidateLimit)?;
1759    report.statistics.candidates += 1;
1760    let queries_before = report.statistics.countermodel_queries;
1761    let countermodels_before = report.statistics.countermodels;
1762    let decision: Result<Decision, Incomplete> = if let Some(certificate) = &shared.certificate {
1763        let mut search = budget.statistics;
1764        let wanted = certificate
1765            .checking_work_bound()?
1766            .min(shared.limits.max_verification_work);
1767        let reservation = budget.quota.reserve(wanted)?;
1768        let allowance = reservation.allowance();
1769        let mut limits = shared.limits;
1770        limits.max_verification_work = allowance;
1771        if allowance < wanted {
1772            // Only a shortage of global work lowers the search ceiling.
1773            // A complete reservation preserves a stricter verification
1774            // ceiling's own error attribution.
1775            limits.search.max_work = search
1776                .work
1777                .checked_add(allowance)
1778                .ok_or(Incomplete::CounterOverflow)?;
1779        }
1780        let verdict = certified::classify(
1781            certificate,
1782            workspace,
1783            &candidate,
1784            limits,
1785            budget.cancellation,
1786            &mut report.statistics,
1787            &mut search,
1788        );
1789        reservation.finish(search.work - budget.statistics.work)?;
1790        budget.statistics = search;
1791        verdict.map(Decision::from)
1792    } else {
1793        membership
1794            .check(
1795                original.theory(),
1796                Some(original),
1797                &candidate,
1798                shared.limits,
1799                budget,
1800                &mut report.statistics,
1801            )
1802            .map(Decision::from)
1803    };
1804    // Query entry is a receipt even when membership checking stops before
1805    // producing a verdict. Publish it before propagating that failure.
1806    Live::add(
1807        &shared.live.countermodel_queries,
1808        report.statistics.countermodel_queries - queries_before,
1809    );
1810    Live::add(
1811        &shared.live.countermodels,
1812        report.statistics.countermodels - countermodels_before,
1813    );
1814    match decision? {
1815        Decision::Stable => Ok(Some(candidate)),
1816        Decision::Refuted => Ok(None),
1817        Decision::Invalid => Err(Incomplete::InvalidWitness),
1818    }
1819}
