3/// Strategy for positive relational joins during eager formula grounding.
4#[derive(Clone, Copy, Debug, Default, PartialEq, Eq)]
5pub enum JoinStrategy {
6    /// Probe the shortest available equality posting and check complete rows.
7    /// A completed flat constraint with a finite arithmetic-totality certificate
8    /// may compose a computed equality domain with the shared table selector.
9    /// This special query keeps the original rows and residual checks; other
10    /// probes remain indexed even after that table has been prepared.
11    #[default]
12    Indexed,
13    /// Reuse finite-table indices for flat positive patterns over completed
14    /// possible support. Structural patterns and support-growth rounds retain
15    /// indexed matching. An exhausted table operation is an error, not fallback.
16    ///
17    /// This explicit strategy has no automatic size or speed threshold. Index
18    /// construction may cost more work or storage than the rows it avoids.
19    Table,
20}

22/// Execution policy for materializing a prepared formula source.
23///
24/// These choices change neither source meaning nor the retained admission
25/// ceilings. They introduce no cancellation or timing contract. Grounding work
26/// observation reports actual operations, including inapplicable table probes.
27#[derive(Clone, Copy, Debug, Default, PartialEq, Eq)]
28pub struct GroundingOptions {
29    /// Positive-join execution strategy; independent of formula/reduct solving.
