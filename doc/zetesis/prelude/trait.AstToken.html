Expand description

A typed token: a view over one token, cast by kind — and, for the two comment wrappers, by the token’s role.

Required Methods§

fn can_cast(kind: SyntaxKind) -> bool

Whether tokens of kind may cast (the role, where it matters, is read at cast).

fn cast(token: SyntaxToken<Asp>) -> Option<Self>

The wrapper over token, when it is of the wrapper’s kind and role.

fn syntax(&self) -> &SyntaxToken<Asp>

The token.
