Shared execution primitives: cancellation, regions, walks and packed words.

These operations carry no program semantics. Their callers establish narrowing soundness and decide answer-set membership through the reduct.

Constants§

POLL_INTERVAL
The most operations a metered computation charges between two observations of its cancellation and deadline flags. A poll reads up to three shared flags; reading them once per this many operations instead of at every operation takes that read out of per-node loops, while at most POLL_INTERVAL - 1 further operations can follow a requested stop.

Traits§

Quota
A caller-owned account granting permits to one metered operation. A reserve refusal grants nothing. A successful reserve grants 1..=wanted; zero is treated as a local work refusal. The cumulative grant retained by one meter must fit u64, including already performed work. The account retains its own typed errors.
Refusal
An error a metered operation reports, classified so that a requested stop takes precedence over every resource refusal at the operation’s exit (Cancellation::metered).
Reservation
An already-admitted whole-kernel allowance and its settlement authority. Obtaining the guard remains the originating account’s operation. Dropping it without committing preserves that account’s policy for unreceipted work. This is ownership of a reservation, not a second work counter.

Functions§

poll_due
Whether admitting amount operations after charged contains an observation boundary. Operation numbers are zero-based multiples of POLL_INTERVAL. A zero amount admits nothing and is never due. Computes no counter sum, so the decision cannot overflow. This is a count boundary; it makes no promise about wall-clock latency or unmetered work.
