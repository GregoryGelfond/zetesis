Module reduction 

Source
Expand description

Pure value reductions and the verdict of a possibly incomplete enumeration.

Reductions combine values; they do not decide membership, retain witnesses, allocate storage or poll control. The caller authenticates accepted members, completed coverage and the successful effects preceding absorption. Ordered instances additionally require the documented pure order on their values.
