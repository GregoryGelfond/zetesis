Expand description

Canonical union of two strictly increasing streams under their value order.

The caller establishes ordering and uniqueness within each input; no validation scan is added. Equal cross-input values keep the left representative, consuming the right occurrence first. Each next operation peeks left then right and performs at most one comparison. The caller must perform any capacity admission before constructing/draining this cursor. The cursor retains only the two input cursors and one lookahead per input; it performs no allocation, copying of owned payload or fallible publication.
