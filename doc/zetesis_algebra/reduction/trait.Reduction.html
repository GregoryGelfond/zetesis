Expand description

A built-in commutative value reduction with an optional absorbing value.

combine is associative and commutative, and unit is its identity. If is_absorbing(value), combining that value with any continuation preserves it. These are the value laws of Zetesis.AxisFolds.Reduction; they do not commute allocations, control observations or resource-failure traces.

Implementations are sealed and statically dispatched. No caller-defined membership or narrowing policy is accepted. The built-in operations perform no allocation or control observation. An ordered instance additionally requires a pure, allocation-free total order consistent with value equality; its value laws hold under that contract, which Rust’s Ord bound alone does not enforce. See Minimum.
