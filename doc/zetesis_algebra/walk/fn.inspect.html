Expand description

Inspect an entry after its caller has completed and accounted narrowing.

Refutation takes precedence over every shape of region. Otherwise a decided region is a leaf, even when unchanged counting is selected. An unchanged open region is counted only below the root; all other open regions split on the entry’s existing preference or highest-open fallback. Counting::Never cannot return Step::Counted.

This is the allocation-free classification shared by the scalar, parallel, slate and published-query schedules. It reads no theory, invokes no semantic checker, consumes no entry, and performs no resource admission. The caller retains its narrowing, receipt-publication and split-admission order. A returned split atom is open in the inspected entry; mutating that interval afterwards requires inspection again before consuming the split.
