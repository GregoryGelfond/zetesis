Expand description

One deque of entries per worker and the walk’s termination record.

An owner pops its newest region and thieves take the oldest. At most one deque lock is held at a time, and none while the gate is waited for. A measured frontier keeps exact receipts of its queued regions; its deques are then changed only through these operations.

Deque 0 holds root; the others start empty. Storage for the deques and the root’s entry is reserved fallibly. The frontier keeps no receipts. As in the existing protocol, callers supply at least one worker.

pub fn requeue( &self, index: usize, entry: E, reserve: impl FnOnce(&mut VecDeque<E>, usize) -> Result<(), R>, ) -> Result<(), (R, E)>

Return a region to worker index’s deque without changing the count: a region taken but neither resolved nor split goes back. The injected reservation is the sole fallible storage effect, as in Self::publish_split_with. Refusal returns the still-owned entry beside the exact error. The caller retains unresolved coverage until it records a terminal stop; only then may it abandon that entry.

pub fn steal(&self, index: usize) -> Option<E>

Take the oldest region of a peer’s deque, trying peers in round-robin order and skipping a busy one, so one owner’s growth cannot block stealing from the others. No two queue locks overlap, and none is waited for.

pub fn wait_for_work<T>( &self, timeout: Duration, try_take: impl FnMut(Attempt) -> Option<T>, ) -> Option<T>

Wait at the gate for a published region or the end of the walk, for at most timeout. Under the gate the waiter registers, fences, and then re-checks the walk’s end and try_take; a region found then is returned at once. None does not mean the walk ended: the caller re-checks. What a region is, a frontier entry or another deque’s item taken beside it, is try_take’s.

pub fn publish_split_with<const N: usize, H>( &self, index: usize, parent: E, reserve: impl FnOnce(&mut VecDeque<E>, usize) -> Result<(), R>, children: impl FnOnce(E) -> ([E; N], H), ) -> Result<H, (R, E)>

Reserve publication storage while retaining the unsplit parent, then construct and publish its children on worker index’s deque.

The injected reservation must admit N entries, as reserve_entries does. children runs only after success, under the same deque lock, so no other publisher can consume the admitted capacity. Prepare expensive lineage copies before calling; move them in children without copying again. The parent remains owned on every reservation refusal.

A binary split grows the unresolved count by one, even when N is one and the returned value retains the other child in hand. Children are pushed in array order. The deque is released before waking an idle worker. No polling or semantic decision is performed by this boundary.

§Errors

Returns the exact reservation refusal and parent without invoking children, publishing an entry or changing the unresolved count.

§Panics

Panics if index is outside the worker deques. A panic or process-level allocation abort in children is outside the fallible reservation contract; callers retain their existing panic/abort handling.

pub fn find_work<T>( &self, idle_wait: Duration, cancellation: &Cancellation, try_take: impl FnMut(Attempt) -> Option<T>, on_stop: impl FnOnce(R), ) -> Option<T>

Look for a region, waiting while any remains. None when the whole frontier is resolved or the walk closed; a cancellation observed while idle is reported through on_stop first. try_take makes one attempt and is told where it runs. Every idle round makes one attempt outside the gate; of every sixteen rounds, the first fifteen then yield and the last waits at the gate, where it attempts once more, and then polls cancellation. idle_wait bounds that wait; a publication or the end of the walk ends it at once.

Source

pub fn release(&self)

Release every deque’s payloads and capacity, once no party can still own or publish work. A measured frontier’s current receipts become its headers alone; its peaks remain.
