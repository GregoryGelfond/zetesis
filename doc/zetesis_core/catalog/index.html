Module catalog 

Source
Expand description

Canonical typed terms and atoms with immutable read snapshots.

A catalog owns identities, not truth. Publishing a selection neither derives its atoms nor establishes answer-set membership. Dense occurrence positions remain distinct from canonical identities: equal atoms may occur at several positions in a supplied program.
