pub const POLL_INTERVAL: u64 = 256;
Expand description

The most operations a metered computation charges between two observations of its cancellation and deadline flags. A poll reads up to three shared flags; reading them once per this many operations instead of at every operation takes that read out of per-node loops, while at most POLL_INTERVAL - 1 further operations can follow a requested stop.
