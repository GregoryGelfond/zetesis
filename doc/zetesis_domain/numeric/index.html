zetesis_domain

Module numeric 

Source
Expand description

Argument domains over neutral producer plans.

One lowering borrows source symbols; another may submit authenticated catalog keys. Logical stops publish no bounds. Reader and allocation failures carry the committed receipt, without a partial analysis. Bounds describe defined values; they do not establish that arithmetic is total or that a binding exists. Requested integer certificates separately establish defined arithmetic over complete-binding domains, without proving a binding or a prefix extension.
