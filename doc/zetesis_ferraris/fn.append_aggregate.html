Expand description

Append a finite, scalar #count/#sum aggregate formula transactionally. Conditions may be arbitrary existing formulas. Each input entry must identify a different complete tuple; callers coalesce duplicate tuple eligibility with OR before calling. #count uses weight one. The caller supplies one evaluated scalar guard and applies default negation as implication to falsum afterward.

Nonnegative weights use shared monotone threshold formulas. Mixed/negative weights use every failing subset’s implication without complementing weights. Neither path introduces semantic atoms or relies on classical-only rewriting. Existing edge topology and condition IDs are checked; atom-universe admission remains the responsibility of crate::Theory::new.

On failure, original nodes and length are restored; reserved capacity may change. The caller owns source origins and should restrict max_nodes to its remaining origin capacity before appending, then record every new node.
