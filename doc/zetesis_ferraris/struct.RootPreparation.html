The two immutable original-answer preparations, sharing one optional budget. A parity refutation absorbs before cardinality preparation is necessary.

pub fn prepare_reserved<Q: Quota + ?Sized>( theory: &Theory, producers: Option<&Producers>, limits: PreparationLimits, cancellation: &Cancellation, meter: &mut Meter<'_, Q>, ) -> PreparationAttempt<Failure<Q::Error>>

Prepare root consequences from the exact original theory and its already complete producer inventory. This never extracts another inventory. Optional limits publish no partial rows or units; global refusals keep their typed error. Spare global work grants are refunded on every exit.

pub fn take_seeded_root(&mut self) -> Option<Region>

Transfer the root seeded by P0’s unique one-atom rows, at most once. None means there were no units, preparation refuted the root, or the caller already took it. Check root_refuted before creating a walk.
