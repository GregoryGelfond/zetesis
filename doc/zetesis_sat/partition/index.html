Module partition 

Source
Expand description

Cardinality consequences conditional on explicitly stated finite premises.

A Plan validates a partition’s shape and computes local lower bounds. It does not establish that an original theory entails the supplied total or group capacities. A caller may restrict complete candidate enumeration only after independently establishing those premises for that original theory. Conditional aggregate groups require a common, established activation; passing their unconditional capacities here does not discharge that obligation.
