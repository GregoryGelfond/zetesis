Crate zetesis_stable 

Source
Expand description

Region search for the stable models of finite general-formula (Ferraris) theories.

Candidates are regions of the theory’s atoms narrowed by its readings, with no clause form; every leaf is a classical model of the theory. Membership is the frozen reduct’s: its proper-subset query is walked as a region tree over the same index, and a countermodel is validated independently before it refutes a candidate. Only original semantic atoms enter minimality.

A hybrid program’s streamed integrity constraints, admitted by zetesis-themelios, are checked over candidate regions by StreamedConstraints through that crate’s checker; no caller code refutes a region or decides an atom. Beyond that check, this crate does not parse, ground, translate source aggregates, invoke an external solver, or claim a refinement proof for its Rust implementation.

The admitted source and its consequence representation enter through streamed_constraints. Candidate composition applies those consequences; frozen membership reads only the Ferraris theory and its own typed state.

check
Check stability through the proper-subset query of the frozen reduct, a region tree narrowed by the reduct’s readings. A countermodel is validated independently before it refutes the candidate. No enumeration of all subsets or invocation of an external solver occurs. max_candidates applies only to StableModels.
check_interpretation
Check and retain an owned candidate against its own immutable theory. Uses exactly crate::check’s original satisfaction and proper-subset search. Search can be exponential; all native bounds and stops apply. The candidate words are moved, not cloned. Caller-supplied verdicts do not enter this operation. An inconclusive result retains the candidate and stop reason.
