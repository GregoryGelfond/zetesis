The statistics validation qualifies from a GPU formula run, as the command line renders them: the exhaustion, route, completion, batching, device and countermodel lines. The search counters come from a real solve of a | b.; the device counters are authored to agree with them under a format-fixture adapter, and claim no physical execution. The command line’s tests fail when its rendering leaves this text, and validation’s tests fail when it cannot qualify it, so neither side can change alone.
