Helpers the zetesis workspace’s tests share over the Ferraris theories.

The crate depends on zetesis-core, zetesis-cpu and zetesis-ferraris alone, so the tests that use it compile nothing beyond them: the device tests of zetesis-wgpu and the integration tests of zetesis-sat and zetesis-ferraris. It is not published or installed.
