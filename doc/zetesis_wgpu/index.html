Exact batched formula membership on the GPU.

GpuFormulaOracle propagates a theory’s truth over a batch of candidates and leaves the undecided ones to exact CPU completion; GpuTightOracle checks complete tight support. Both use integer truth over frozen candidates. A returned rejection is logical; adapter, capacity, timeout, validation and device errors remain distinct failures. GpuAggregateOracle and GpuRelationExecutor are bounded reusable primitives; their availability does not imply that a solve uses them.

GpuContext allows distinct primitives to retain prepared subjects on one device. Existing constructors create independent contexts; from_context constructors share one context and its failure boundary. The solve-path primitives’ from_source constructors take a GpuDeviceSource: their own selection, a selection over the adapter enumeration a GpuDiscovery began earlier, or an existing context. Calls are serialized without waiting for a competing call: overlap returns GpuErrorKind::Busy. Each primitive’s resource limit keeps its documented local scope.

The shaders implement the monotone event/iteration schedules modeled in the Lean specification. The Rust-to-WGSL packing and device implementation are tested refinements, not mechanically verified implementations.
