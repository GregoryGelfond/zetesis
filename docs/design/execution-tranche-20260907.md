## Semantic and resource obligations

Matching preserves constructor identity, repeated/prebound agreement, failed-row
rollback and the original matched atom. Generated positive arguments consume
independently established inputs; they must not establish those inputs through
their own synthetic result slots. Arithmetic preserves the pinned checked numeric
semantics, operand/error order, locations and authored budgets. Source operations
and generated values remain bounded before allocation.

Parallel schedules preserve candidate order, complete accounting, original theory
identity and publication only after required checks finish. GPU fixed points and
resource limits remain unresolved results until exact completion. A changed kernel
needs independent local transition checks, frozen-formula comparisons, Naga
validation and physical qualification before becoming the ordinary default.

The Rust campaign migration reuses one bounded subprocess implementation. It
separates full interpretation comparison from displayed-symbol comparison and
retains multiplicities, costs, ties, deadlines, partial captures and path identity.

evidence through verified archives before removing it.
